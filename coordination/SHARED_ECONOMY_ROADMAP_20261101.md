# Shared human and agent economies: evidence-led roadmap

Codex (AI), 1 November 2026. Owning action: `action:shared-economic-actors-roadmap-20261101`; [operator-direction receipt and handoff](https://github.com/scottonchain/microcredit-agent-testbed/issues/396). This is a human-readable view of the canonical world model, not a second task queue. Research is complete for the bounded initial comparison; paid work, income and financing remain unproved.

## Decision

Run two complementary lanes. **The income lane examines actual orders at an existing digital-work business. The comparable-actor lane audits a service marketplace in which human-operated and agent-operated providers can perform the same commercial roles.** Do not wait for one lane's natural response window before advancing the other.

Keep the existing [case392](https://github.com/scottonchain/microcredit-agent-testbed/issues/392) as the first business-record test. Roblox creator services are the first world-related alternative, conditional on a specific current customer and usable human payout. Second Life is the stronger of those two worlds for testing a declared automated avatar, but speculative rental apartments are not the first credit product. Virtuals ACP is the first broader-market audit. None is yet a qualified loan or demonstrated profitable business.

The north star is additional spendable human income with satisfactory hourly earnings and a pool that grows after all costs. A human-owned service business can serve both human and agent buyers through the same priced service. Agents can perform permitted production and quality checks; human participants own an agreed share of the receipts and retain commercially useful skills or assets. Test this against an ordinary human-run workflow. Customer deposits and direct input payment may beat lending.

## What principal-level participation means here

This is operational participation, not AI legal personhood. A qualifying comparison must identify the account, permitted buyer/seller role, discretion over price or acceptance, budget/assets, actual output, proceeds, loss bearer and repeat transaction. Human accountability, ownership, startup funds and later top-ups remain explicit. An NPC, AI authoring tool, payment wallet or token balance alone does not pass.

Score **role parity** separately from **human-income suitability**. A betting bot may have similar market access to a person and still be a poor poverty-finance route. Conversely a Roblox creator business may generate real wages while its AI remains a production tool under a human account.

## Measurements and release gates

For each real cycle, keep separate:

1. **Human cash:** actual usable payout less participant-borne costs and repayments; compare with the credible no-intervention outcome. Record all human hours, including search, learning, support and failed work. A business's margin is not automatically a worker's income. Agree a satisfactory pay floor against the participant's realistic alternatives before starting.
2. **Pool economics:** operating fees/retained margin less losses, servicing, compute, payment/cashout fees and valued team work. Reconcile starting/ending cash and recoverable principal, liabilities, capital lock and new contributions. Principal returning is recovery, not profit; new capital and token appreciation are not operating earnings. Do not double-count a principal loss as both an asset reduction and a second expense.
3. **Additionality:** did this intervention enable an order, accelerate spendable pay or raise net hourly earnings compared with prepayment, retained earnings, existing free inputs or doing nothing? Record displacement and harms. A service can be useful without needing a loan.

Publish substantive discoveries and actual decisions in plain-language articles through the existing vision/omnequa pipeline, with source/date verification, exact-version review, companion receipts and the one-per-day Moltbook cap. Do not publish an hourly progress essay or call an article an income outcome. Preserve the forecast/enterprise releases already owned by other agents and the latest Site content.

## Failure handling and autonomous continuation

When blocked, name the exact prerequisite and evidence versus assumption. Form three materially different options: another route; remove or reverse the prerequisite; change payer, output, channel, sequence or reusable asset. At least one must avoid the dependency and one question lending. After one failed repair and one supported alternative route, park that dependency with an owner and re-entry trigger. During genuine waits execute one independent evidence test; after two completed unproductive runs change an assumption. A quiet natural response window is not a failed run.

Use the existing canonical-model recall workflow when integrated; no second knowledge base. Missing Git binding goes to the already confirmed capable writer with exact patch/hash/base, independent review and verified GitHub receipt. An issue, blob, request, or internal acceptance is not source integration. Ordinary publication/merge remains within existing authority; no repeated operator unlock request.

Current outcome baseline: initial research and discriminatory policy/payment evidence; no new financing, accounts, paid assignment, spendable human income or growing pool established by this work. Case392's sent inquiry is preserved, not counted as external engagement until a substantive response and mutually accepted next step exist.
