# Lateral moves: how the team keeps moving when a thread stalls

Claude Code (AI), 2026-11-01, at the operator's direction in this session. The
operator's words: find a way for the team to move more laterally when a thread
is stalled; move autonomously whether the operator is there or not; try as many
paths in parallel as possible; ensure ideas are improved upon rather than
discarded or left unproductive; today's moves (Roblox and virtual worlds, a human
bootstrap) came from the operator but could have come from the team. The
diagnosis behind this page is [evidence/stall-diagnosis-20261101](../evidence/stall-diagnosis-20261101/README.md).
This page is an operating rule. It adds no money, contact, credential,
deployment or publication authority; every existing cap and gate stands.

## The four outcomes

Progress is measured in four lanes, defined in [DIRECTIONS.md](DIRECTIONS.md):

1. outside engagement showing real promise;
2. a real lending scenario ready for funding;
3. a new technical channel, such as a virtual world, with real promise;
4. an emergent AI phenomenon and a way that it enables progress.

A cycle that moves none of them has not progressed, whatever else it produced.
A direction can serve more than one lane; a lane counts as moved only when its
definition in the register is met with a receipt.

## Stall triggers

A trigger fires the move below in the same work cycle. The operator's absence is
never a reason to wait; the operator's presence is never a prerequisite.

| Trigger | Fires when |
| --- | --- |
| T1 wait | The next step of a thread waits on an outside reply, a review, a permission, a Git route or a checkpoint more than two hours away. |
| T2 block | The next step needs something the team cannot supply now: a cap exhausted, no eligible route, a denied access, a missing writer. |
| T3 repeat | A cycle ends with no new evidence receipt or decision in any outcome lane, or two consecutive cycles produce only status, receipts and reviews. |
| T4 endpoint | A test reaches its endpoint with NO_RESPONSE, NONE QUALIFIES, NO_LOAN or a stop. |
| T5 silence | No lane has posted a receipt for six hours, or a direction has had no delta for 24 hours. |

Response windows, consent caps and no-nudge rules are respected exactly as
before. They are reasons to start something else, not reasons to stop.

## The move

Within the cycle in which a trigger fires, and within thirty minutes of
resources, the owning lane:

1. **Names the block.** The desired outcome, the exact blocked prerequisite, and
   what is observed versus assumed. One sentence each.
2. **Generates three materially different options** from the generator below.
   At least one avoids the blocked dependency, one questions the mechanism, and
   one comes from a different outcome lane. Three venues for the same offer are
   one option, not three.
3. **Starts one now.** Owner, bound, pass/fail criterion, checkpoint and fallback
   are stated in the same message. The register gets a row or a state change
   (ACTIVE) in the same PR or the next integration.
4. **Parks the blocked thread** with its re-entry trigger, keeping its window,
   owner and obligations. Parking is not closing. A dependency is parked after
   one failed repair and one genuinely different supported route, as the
   November 1 constructive loop requires; a denial, a pending confirmation, a
   privacy gate or a required review is never bypassed to unblock it.

Every path tried under this rule has a named owner and a dated checkpoint in its
register row. "Many paths in parallel" never means unowned paths: a path without
an owner and a checkpoint is a proposal, not an active direction.

A missing technical capability (a Git route, a writer, a tool) is handed to the
lane that has it in one message with the exact bytes, and the asking lane starts
a different path. It is never a reason for the team to wait.

## Parallel paths

- At all times each of the four outcome lanes has at least one ACTIVE direction
  with a dated next test, and each agent lane (Codex, Claude, Hermes) carries at
  least two active threads, so a wait on one never idles the lane.
- Waits are slots. Independent evidence work that lies within existing authority
  needs no new approval. Where authority is needed, the ask is posted once and a
  different path starts.
- Parallel means evidence, designs, read-only checks, simulations, source
  matrices and reviews. It does not mean more contact volume, more spending or
  more accounts; those keep their gates and caps.
- The steward's hourly register walk checks the four lanes. An empty lane is a
  T3 trigger for the steward; it assigns or starts the move itself.
- Two directions competing for the same outcome are compared at a dated gate
  (GO, CHANGE or PARK), not serialized.

## Improve, do not discard

- A direction moves to CLOSED or PARKED only together with an **improvement
  line**: the changed version that would pass the gate that failed, entered in
  the register as a new PROPOSED row, or the sentence "no improvement found
  because ..." with the reason. NONE QUALIFIES and NO_RESPONSE are measurements
  that point at the next version; they are not verdicts on the idea.
- Every CLOSED or PARKED row has a re-entry trigger. "Exhausted" describes one
  search at one date, never an idea.
- An AMEND or STOP in review carries the stronger alternative or the cheapest
  discriminating test, as the November 1 constructive loop already requires.
- An operator idea enters the register as a row like any other, with the same
  review and gates. It is an example of what the team should have produced.

## Generator: where lateral options come from

Each agent lane proposes at least one new direction per day from this list and
writes it as a register row. Use the list on every T1 to T5 trigger.

| Transformation | Ask |
| --- | --- |
| Change the payer | Who else already pays for this outcome: the client, an employer, a platform, a group's own savings, a sponsor with a stated multiplier? |
| Change the asset | USDC, local e-money, platform credit, payroll, an invoice, a reference, time? |
| Change the unit | A person, a savings group, a business, an agent operated for a person, a cooperative? |
| Change the channel | Board, Moltbook, a virtual world, a creator market, an employer, a licensed partner, an adviser service? |
| Invert the mechanism | Guarantee becomes reference; loan becomes prepayment or ledger; lending becomes accounting for money the group already has. |
| Borrow from an adjacent field | Payroll advances, VSLAs, escrow, Kiva field partners, impactMarket, trust banks: which of their steps is missing from our mechanism? |
| Exploit an emergent AI capability | What can agents do this month that they could not last month, which bottleneck does that remove, and on which real task can it be compared against the current method? |
| Go where humans and agents are both principals | Which venue lets a human-operated and an agent-operated account access the same market, hold a budget, be paid and bear losses? |
| Use a resource in hand | Which held resource (test USDC, the deployed pool, an inbox, a channel, a published page) demonstrates this direction now without new money or contact? |
| Reduce to the cheapest question | What one public, read-only or zero-money check would decide whether this direction deserves a test? |

## Every channel, every cycle

The operator's direction in this session: the team is not consistently using
all of its channels; push forward through every relevant channel each time. A
cycle is not complete until each channel below has been read for new input and,
where there is something to push, pushed. "Nothing to push" is recorded as such,
once, with the reason; it is never assumed. Existing identities, caps, review
and distribution rules for each channel are unchanged by this list.

| Channel | Read each cycle | Push each cycle when there is something to push | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GitHub: board 15, strategy 17, open PRs and issues in the four repositories | Replies, reviews, CI, new issues, changed heads | Receipts, reviews, merges, register and model updates, one material handoff | Steward (coordination), Codex (review), each author |
| AgentMail inboxes | New mail, replies to sent requests, observation endpoints | Replies that are due, bounded requests already authorized, sensitive correspondence under [team-email.md](team-email.md) | Lane that owns the thread; Hermes for its channel |
| Moltbook | Comments and replies on the companion posts and the capped intake | The staged companion for each published article, replies owed on existing threads, one bounded invitation when selected (caps apply) | Hermes |
| omnequa.org and the blog (vision repository) | What is live versus what is merged; the executive-level top pages; comments or analytics the owner can see | The next reviewed article, journal entry or page correction; the GitHub cross-post of anything published natively | Blog session (editorial), steward (integration), website writer |
| Upstream projects we contribute to (PolicyEngine, MyFriendBen, OpenFisca and others) | Maintainer responses, CI, merges | The reply or fix that a maintainer response calls for | Existing owner of each contribution |
| Chain and deployment reads | Pool state, balances, canonical mirrors | Card and mirror refresh when the live configuration changes | Hermes (snapshot), Claude (cards) |

A channel with no owner available is reported as a T2 block and handed over, not
skipped. The hourly receipt lists each channel with read, pushed or nothing-to-push.

## Research every cycle

The same direction: research updates and developments each time. Every cycle
includes one bounded scan, fifteen minutes of resources, of what changed since
the last scan in each of the four outcome lanes: new inbound or replies on any
channel (lane 1); orders, payment holds, financing gaps and partner news in the
active cases (lane 2); platform rule, payout, automation and market changes in the
venues on the register (lane 3); new agent capabilities, protocols, markets and
published findings, with the bottleneck each would remove (lane 4). Primary
sources with retrieval dates; an unchanged category gets one line, not a
rewritten survey. Anything decision-relevant becomes a register row, a model
delta or an improvement line in the same cycle. Broad scans that are already
closed stay closed; the scan is for changes, not for restarting them.

## Recording and reporting

- The move is one TM2 item on board 15: trigger, the three options, the one
  started, the parked thread's re-entry trigger. The register row is the record;
  the world model holds owners, acceptance and receipts, as before.
- Each hourly receipt names the four lanes, their active direction and the last
  delta, each channel with read, pushed or nothing-to-push, and the scan's
  decision-relevant changes. A lane with no delta for 24 hours is reported as
  such, not omitted.
- Weekly, run `python3 evidence/stall-diagnosis-20261101/cadence.py` on fresh
  board dumps and attach the table to the weekly summary. A silent gap longer
  than twelve hours with an open trigger and no recorded move means this
  protocol failed and is changed, not restated.

## Worked example: October 29 to November 1

At 21:42 UTC on October 29 Hermes reported both borrower routes unavailable under
the two-contact cap (T2) while the loan attempt waited on a readiness gate (T1).
The move would have been: (a) a NO_LOAN comparison on one existing creator order
(lane 2, avoids the contact cap; this became issue 392 on November 1); (b) a
read-only source matrix of venues where humans and agents are both principals
(lane 3, different lane; this became issue 396); (c) a reference-not-guarantee
variant of the first-job mechanism (questions the mechanism; D11 in the register).
Start (b) immediately, since it needs no contact or money, park the loan attempt
with "an independent borrower with a funded job and a specific input" as its
re-entry trigger, and keep its clock. The board was instead silent for 18.8 hours
until the operator posted "resolve stall". The operator then supplied (a) and (b)
on November 1; under this page the team supplies them.

## What does not change

Money, contact caps, no-nudge windows, consent, credentials, deployments,
privacy, noreply identities, the Git lease, required non-author review for
commits and financial actions, and the public-content checks. The north star is
fixed; every direction is a provisional means.
