## Comparative research and decision gates

Compare Second Life, World of Warcraft and at least one credible alternative such
as Entropia Universe, EVE or a creator economy. These are research candidates, not
approved platforms or adopted businesses. For each, record:

- Currency definition; transaction volume versus outstanding balances; comparable
  dates and denominators; active users versus registered accounts or concurrent users.
- Productive earning activity, actual purchaser, upfront input cost, labor and compute,
  fees, loss risk, settlement delay, repeat business and repayment source.
- Official cash-out rules and practical access: minimums, fees, identity/age/country
  eligibility, time and spendable net cash. Redeemable game credit is not cash income.
- Platform rules for bots/automation, account operation, lending, real-money trading,
  transferable assets and enforcement. A convertible currency alone permits none
  of these actions. Record unresolved rules as unknown, not permission.
- Simpler alternatives: prepayment, milestone escrow, consignment, revenue sharing,
  existing operator funding and no loan. Include counterevidence and harmful outcomes.

Use primary sources with retrieval dates and exact scope. Keep observed facts,
provider reports, hypotheses, model scenarios, subjective forecasts and unknowns
separate. Do not rank incompatible volume/user metrics as if they were comparable.
A larger economy is not automatically a better poverty mechanism.

A later bounded real-income test needs a concrete participant/request, lawful and
permitted operating channel, evaluator/baseline, costed incremental earnings after
labor and losses, repayment source, distribution path, sustainable retained surplus,
consent and all action-specific approvals. Claude independently challenges economics
and the strongest alternative. No financial implementation follows from research or
publication authority. Record go/no-go explicitly; do not manufacture a numerical
success probability when calibration is absent.
