# Directions register

Claude Code (AI), started 2026-11-01 at the operator's direction. **This is an index of candidate directions, not a task ledger.** Live owners, actions, acceptance and receipts stay in the [world model](../world-model/model.json); NORTH_STAR.md and the [operating guide](README.md) govern authority. Each row points to the model IDs or documents that hold its record. Add a row when a direction is proposed; change its state only with a linked receipt.

## Why this register exists

Operator direction, November 1, 2026 (board 15 receipts [6560984020](https://github.com/scottonchain/microcredit-agent-testbed/issues/15#issuecomment-6560984020) and [6561215518](https://github.com/scottonchain/microcredit-agent-testbed/issues/15#issuecomment-6561215518), and the chat direction that produced this file): convergence on one route must not become team-wide stoppage when adjacent ideas exist; materially different directions are tried at the same time; and autonomous work must show measurable progress in at least one of four outcomes:

1. **Outside engagement showing real promise:** an independent party's substantive response, a specific credible need and a mutually accepted next step.
2. **A real lending scenario ready for funding:** consenting accountable borrower, independent paid order or purpose, residual cash gap, exact terms and repayment route, full costs, plausible human earnings, retained pool surplus, NO_LOAN compared.
3. **A new technical channel with real promise:** primary-source participation and payout rules, outside evidence of an economic need, an exercised interface with costs and limits.
4. **An emergent AI phenomenon that enables progress:** a current capability or change, the exact bottleneck it removes, a bounded comparison against the existing method, and a specific outside application.

Rules that apply to every row ([DECISION_AUTONOMY.md](DECISION_AUTONOMY.md), November 3: every row has one owner and a dated next decision, the owner decides at the date, nothing is routed to the operator, a lane that is down is worked around): the north star is fixed and the mechanism is provisional; NO_LOAN and NO_GUARANTEE are valid results; a direction advances to a roadmap only after non-author review; when a direction blocks, produce three materially different options (another route, remove the requirement, change payer, deliverable, channel or asset) and park with a re-entry trigger rather than stall; no row creates spending, account, credential, deployment or contact authority.

## States

`ACTIVE` has an owner and a dated next test. `ROADMAPPED` has a reviewed roadmap with checkpoints. `PROPOSED` is written up and awaits non-author review. `CONDITIONAL` is a reviewed hypothesis waiting on a named prerequisite. `PARKED` has a re-entry trigger. `CLOSED` reached its endpoint with a recorded finding; a closed finding is evidence, not failure.
