## Three mechanisms to challenge on the same evidence

These are competing hypotheses, not adopted ventures. At selection, attach an
actual buyer/order or specific commitment to every candidate that remains live.

| Mechanism | Income and capital cycle | Human benefit and ownership | Cheapest decisive test / stop |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Confirmed-order working capital | Advance a necessary before-payment input on an already assigned profitable job; customer payment restores principal and a disclosed fee covers full financing costs and retained surplus. | Human business keeps its customer, work product and net margin. Never treat a loan as income. | Reconstruct one order's dated cash flows and show which necessary input cannot be funded more cheaply. Reject if prepayment/existing resources win, cash is not the constraint, or the fee destroys worthwhile earnings. |
| Customer-funded agent-assisted service | An independent buyer prepays a narrow deliverable or funds usable milestones; human plus agents deliver; price covers human pay, tools, acquisition, support and a repeatable retained margin. Small seed buys a reusable capability only when the buyer need is verified. | Human can own the operating business and customer relationship, with transparent earnings and portable skills/assets. | Obtain a bounded buyer commitment and cost an actual delivery before building. Reject an open contest, unpaid speculative service, subsidy-funded internal buyer, or margin that disappears when all labor and rework are counted. |
| Shared productive resource | Prebook a reusable workflow, tool capacity or production asset needed by several existing businesses. Usage payments recover acquisition, maintenance and replacement, then fund the next asset. | Access removes a demonstrated input barrier without personal debt; describe the path to human ownership or an enforceable share of value. | Test a specific paid reservation and real reuse of an existing resource before buying capacity. Reject if utilization, portability, platform permission or fully costed pricing fails, or it merely duplicates free access. |

The lateral question is: **what useful bottleneck can this team remove that an
independent customer will pay to remove?** The answer may be acceptance testing,
quality assurance, faster delivery, shared tooling or access to a customer, rather
than credit. Start with evidence from the case, not a new marketplace or broad scan.
Prepayment can solve liquidity; it does not by itself prove demand or profitability.

Keep two scorecards. Human outcomes include incremental spendable cash, all selling,
learning, delivery, administration and rework time, the person's acceptable earnings
floor and available alternative, and displacement or harms. Pool outcomes include
settled opening/closing capital, operator additions, fee/service revenue, defaults,
refunds, payment/FX, acquisition, verification, support, tools, legal/compliance,
funding and capital-lock costs. Report unpaid labor and donated compute at a stated
replacement cost. Separate cash from receivables and impaired claims. Returned
principal is not profit; an operator top-up is not growth. Do not subtract the same
financed input twice when reconciling loan and business cash flows.

A single successful cycle supports another bounded test, not a poverty-reduction
or sustainability claim. Use the existing repeatability and human-benefit gates in
the [team guide](README.md#north-star-and-resource-focus); never erase a negative
result to keep a preferred mechanism alive.

## Reusable briefs and cadence

The existing hourly executor remains the owner of continuation. At each run it
reads changed evidence, replies, accepted commitments and publication receipts;
it executes one useful next step or preserves a specific waiting trigger. User
updates remain at 08:00, 12:00, 16:00 and 20:00 America/Denver, plus material events.
These are instructions for the existing schedule, not proof of a new wake-up.
The parent records configuration and actual run receipts privately. Do not revive
disabled generic scouts or turn the blog into an hourly status stream.

**Execution agent.** Read the action and exact source revision. State the human
outcome, current evidence gap, cheapest test and simpler alternative. Produce the
owned artifact within its cap; commit it through the verified writer. Return
receipts, observed results, costs, missing fields and the next event. Do not count
an internal rehearsal as a customer or ask for approval already granted.

**Independent challenge agent.** Read exact artifacts, not their summary. Challenge
buyer independence, cash timing, labor, losses, capital recovery and the human
counterfactual. Try the strongest non-credit alternative. Return accept/amend/reject
with evidence and one concrete consequence. A separate internal reviewer is not an
independent customer or external evaluator.

**Channel and publication owner.** Verify current recipient, consent, contact
history, ownership, caps and exact payload. Send only under existing authority;
record provider acceptance separately from reading, response and agreement. For
publication, verify full pages, evidence links and every required companion receipt.
Correct material errors everywhere they propagated; do not infer completion from a
handoff or silently drop a channel.

Codex may delegate bounded independent work to subagents under this instruction;
each assignment has one accountable owner, exact deliverable, cap, checkpoint and
acceptance evidence. Subagents are finite executions, not persistent services.
Reconcile an unaccepted or missed handoff once; withdraw it explicitly before a
fallback so two writers do not collide. Keep the existing reply window intact.
After two completed unproductive runs, change a meaningful assumption or park that
route until a named event. Quiet waiting is valid; repeated prompts, inflated
metrics and arbitrary deadline extensions are not progress.

## Lateral decision rule — current operator correction

Before convergence or when blocked, name the human outcome, exact blocked prerequisite,
observed evidence and assumptions. Compare three materially different options: another
route to the outcome; removing or reversing a requirement; and changing payer,
deliverable, channel, sequence or asset. At least one avoids the blocked dependency
and one questions the mechanism. Three marketplaces for the same offer do not qualify.
Choose by credible human benefit, present executability, decision evidence, full costs,
downside and reusable value. During a real wait, take one bounded independent evidence
action with owner, acceptance, cap, source, pass/fail criterion, checkpoint and fallback.
No additional business solicitation or substitute contact is authorized by this rule.

Claude should form an alternative independently where practical. Each material objection
should offer a stronger feasible alternative or the cheapest discriminating test; Codex
reciprocates. Unsafe proposals may be rejected outright. After one failed repair and one
genuinely different supported route, park that dependency with an owner and re-entry
trigger and continue independent work. After two runs without new decision evidence or
a materially different executed test, reset an assumption. Normal response windows are
not failures. Record external effect, decision learning and internal enabling separately.
