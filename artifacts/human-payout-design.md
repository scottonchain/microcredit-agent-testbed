# Human Access Design — First Pilot

Version: v2 draft 2026-10-06

Interface: Hosted work interface. Person sees assigned task, reward and required effort; receives capped AI/tool access; checks/supplies task-specific information; authorizes delivery; receives agreed earnings.

Role and effort: Useful role with realistic effort. No unnecessary human work. No passive income promise.

Funding: Resource access sponsored under explicit budget. Person owes no personal debt if experiment fails. Participant reward funded and disclosed rejection/remediation terms provided before work starts. Guarantee/subsidy separated from uncertain customer revenue.

Eligibility: Start with one supported jurisdiction, opt-in adult who can receive payment. Example design case: eligible U.S. participant with existing supported bank/account. Verify actual asset/network, fees, minimums, identity requirements and cash-out before calling token balance human benefit. Do not collect private identity information on GitHub.

Payout route: Specify one narrow route with current eligibility, minimums and fees. Do not infer lending suitability for humans from agent history.

Success criteria: Actual spendable net earnings, time required, support cost and errors recorded; then repeat opportunity.

Constraints: No MFI partnership prerequisite. Later route for people without bank access must be proven separately.
