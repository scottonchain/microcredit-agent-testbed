# Costed Job Card — Sample

Candidate: Public-data cleanup sample

Customer payment: Unknown — no independent buyer assignment verified.
Worker reward: To be stated before assignment. Separate from pool interest/fees.
Model/tool use: Existing paid resources credit as asset, not cash repayment. Inference cost estimate to be measured in rehearsal with capped authorized budget.
Transaction/venue fees: To be specified per actual payout route.
Support/rework: Estimate from rehearsal errors and remediation time.
Fixed setup allocation: State separately. Do not describe service as self-sustaining if subsidy remains necessary.
Maximum loss: Capped authorized budget for rehearsal. No personal debt for human pilot.
Cash timing: Advance/sponsor/credit compared. Model rejection/delay considered. Actual contract interest grace applied, not assumed tiny same-day interest.

Unknowns:
- Actual buyer reward and funding origin
- Real inference cost per job
- Payout route fees, minimums, identity requirements

Observed costs:
- Rehearsal script runtime negligible on testbed
- Provider credit unused remains asset

Decision rule: Demonstrate why financing benefits borrower compared with available alternatives. Do not infer demand or borrowing consent from willingness to do paid work.
