Script: `../research/calibration-v3-replay-archive/run_acceptance_in_image_rootfs.sh <layer.tar.gz> <layer_sha256> <archive.tar.gz> <archive_sha256> <seed> <key_salt_hex> <workdir>` (with `rootfs_diag.py` next to it).
