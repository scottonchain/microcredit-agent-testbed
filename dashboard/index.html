Money in, money out, money back

This is a dated Base Sepolia testnet ledger, not real money or evidence of people helped. Gross interest is split between fees, reserve allocations and lender assets. Figures come from a committed snapshot; the browser does not read the chain. Inventory and stock reconciliation uses provider-supplied logs, not independent proof of complete event history. Balanced omitted deposits and withdrawals can pass those checks.

Human outcomes are not measured

Real-person profile ingestion and publication are disabled. Wallet-linked chain data is pseudonymous and permanent. Removing a display or revoking an attestation cannot erase history. A profile is not proof of a human borrower or valid consent.
