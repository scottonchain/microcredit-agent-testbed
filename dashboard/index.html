The pool today

Money in, money out, money back

Lenders put test USDC into one pool on Base. Borrowers draw small loans from it and repay with interest; interest that comes back grows the pool. Every figure here is read from the public ledger: a committed snapshot first, then the chain itself each time the page is opened.

Who the money reaches

Anonymized borrower profiles, on-chain

With the borrower's consent, the credit check records a coarse profile on-chain as an attestation tied to the loan: country, region, occupation group, loan purpose and an income band. No name, no exact place, no wallet-to-person link. Each borrower can revoke it.
