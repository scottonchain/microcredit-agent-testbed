# Ali, 61, tailor. Cracked screen, clumsy thumb. Report.

## 1. What I wanted
About $6 to top up my prepaid meter. Pay part now, the rest later. I came back 35 days later, late, and wanted to put it right.

## 2. What happened, step by step
- Opened the link. Big writing, good. But it says "practice money, nothing here is a real loan" (001-look.png). My friend said it was a loan app.
- Tapped Get started. "What do you need?" page with big buttons (003-wait_5s.png).
- Tapped Other, typed 6. "$6 is more than the $5 maximum." Took $5.
- Tapped $5, A bill, In 30 days; all three went green (008-tap_In_30_days.png). It said: $5 now, $0.04 at most, no other fees. Clear.
- Asked for the loan. The desk (an AI) answered in under 15 seconds: yes, about $5.04 by Dec 6 (010-wait_15s.png).
- Tapped Borrow $5.00 now. "Done. You owe $5.00, due by Dec 7" (011-tap_Borrow_5_00_now.png). Wallet $5.10.
- Pay back, Pay only part, typed 2, Pay this part. "You paid $2.00. Still owed: $3.00" (016-tap_Pay_this_part.png).
- Put the phone away 35 days. Came back: "You owe today $3.05. Late by 5 days" (018-days_35.png).
- Pay back, Pay back everything. "Loan closed. You paid $3.04 now and $2.00 earlier" (020-tap_Pay_back_everything.png). Wallet $0.06, can borrow $5.02.
- Opened the Receipt: codes, no amount (021-tap_Receipt.png).
- Looked in More and Help for what being late means. Nothing (025-tap_Help.png).
- Asked the desk. "No problem, no fine, fully repaid" (031-wait_15s.png). Put the phone down.

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1. Start screen. I expected a loan for my meter; I saw "test dollars, nothing to buy with it". Honest, but then what is it for? Slowed me.
- F2. Amount stops at $5; I needed $6. Annoyed me.
- F3. Pay back page. "Pay this part" is a pale button under a big green "Pay back everything" (014-tap_Pay_only_part.png). With my screen, one slip pays it all. Annoyed and worried me.
- F4. After paying $2 it still said "about $3.04 by the due date", the same 4 cents as on $5. Tiny.
- F5. Late screen. "Late by 5 days. Pay back now." then "about $3.05 by the due date if you wait until then" - the due date had passed; nonsense. Worse: nothing said whether being late costs me or marks me, not there, not in Help. I had to ask the desk. Slowed me.
- F6. Receipt. "from wallet 0xab66…6946", "recorded as 0x7587d62f…", "it may first check that you are not a robot". Means nothing to me, and no amount on it. Annoyed me.
- F7. Credit desk after the loan was closed: the old "Borrow $5.00 now" button was still there, now saying "by Jan 11, 2028". I only came to ask a question; a clumsy tap takes a second loan. Annoyed me.
- F8. Due date: Dec 6 from the desk, Dec 7 on the Done box. Tiny.
- F9. Words: wallet, recovery code, test network, stake, backing. "Stake" I still do not follow. Tiny.

## 4. Did I get the loan?
Yes, $5 (not $6). I paid $2 the same day and $3.04 five days after it was due. The screen says I owe nothing, my wallet has $0.06 and I can borrow $5.02. Nothing is due.

## 5. Anything unsafe or dishonest
Nothing dishonest: the yellow "practice" banner is on every screen and the fingerprint just worked. Two things felt uneasy: "what you write is public on the test network", and the big Borrow button left under the chat after I was done.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. On the late screen, say plainly what being late means (fine or not, mark or not) and drop the "by the due date" line once the date is past.
2. Make "Pay this part" as big and clear as "Pay back everything", or ask "pay everything, sure?" first.
3. Once the loan is closed, take the old Borrow button off the desk page, and put the amount in plain words on the receipt instead of codes.
