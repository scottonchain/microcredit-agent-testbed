# Ravi, 24, market seller: test report

## 1. What I wanted
About $8 for ingredients for my weekend food sale. I asked for $20 to be safe, to pay back after 3 days when the sale money comes. I used the link on my work laptop (Windows), not a phone.

## 2. What happened, step by step
1. Opened the link. Big screen looked fine: one card in the middle, a square code on the right for "also on your phone" (001-look.png). Yellow bar: practice money, not real.
2. Tapped "Get started". "One moment", then "your wallet is ready". No sign-up, no password. Fast.
3. "How much? (up to $5)". Tapped "Other", typed 20. Red text: "$20 is more than the $5 maximum" (005-type_e_g_3_75.png). Big button stayed grey.
4. Tapped "Write to the credit desk in your own words", wrote that I sell food and need 20 dollars, pay in 3 days.
5. "AI credit officer" answered in about 15 seconds: limit is $5, take $5, it put a few cents in my wallet for the interest, pay in 30 days is fine (009-wait_15s.png).
6. Tapped "Borrow $5.00 now". "Done" box: I owe $5.00, due Dec 3, 2026 (010-tap_Borrow_5_00_now.png). Wallet $5.10.
7. Came back 3 days later. Wallet still "You owe today $5.00", "due in 27 days" (012-days_3.png).
8. Tapped "Pay back": "owe $5.00, borrowed $5.00 plus 0.38 cents of interest so far" (013-tap_Pay_back.png).
9. Tapped "Pay back everything". "Loan closed. You paid $5.00 plus 0.38 of a cent of interest. Wallet now $0.10. You owe nothing" (014-tap_Pay_back_everything.png).
10. Tapped "Tell us how it went", sent a short note, "Thank you". Closed the app.

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1. Amount screen: I expected to ask for $20, or at least $8. I saw "$5 maximum", and the officer said the same. $5 does not buy my ingredients. Did not block the test, but blocks my real need. Severity: slowed me.
- F2. Pay back screen: "0.38 cents of interest". Is that 38 cents or less than one cent? The big number said $5.00 so I guessed tiny, but I was not sure until the Done box. Severity: annoyed me.
- F3. Wallet after 3 days: "Interest grows a little each day" but "You owe today" still $5.00, same as day one. I thought the clock had not moved. Severity: tiny.
- F4. Words I skipped: "wallet", "test network", "backed by The credit desk", "Who vouches for you". Severity: tiny.
- F5. "Your credit limit $5.00" with a "Credit desk" button under it: I did not know if tapping it would raise my limit. Did not try. Severity: tiny.

## 4. Did I get the loan? Did I pay it back?
Yes, I got $5 (not $20). Yes, I paid it all back after 3 days. Screen says I owe nothing, nothing due, I can borrow again. $0.10 of test dollars left, which the desk gave me.

## 5. Anything unsafe, like real money, or dishonest
- The yellow bar says the whole time it is practice money, so it never felt like real money. Good.
- The desk "put a few cents in my wallet" without me asking. Friendly but strange: who pays that, and in a real loan who pays the interest?
- "What you write to the desk is public on the test network." I wrote no name, but a market seller may not notice that line.
- Face/fingerprint "is the key" and it never asked me anything on a work laptop. The first screen says remove the wallet from Help on a shared computer; I forgot, and the app did not remind me at the end.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. Say on the first screen, very big: "only $5 in this test, not for real shopping", so I do not waste time typing 20 and writing to the desk.
2. Write interest in money, not "0.38 cents": "less than 1 cent". If it grows each day, show it growing, or do not say it grows.
3. After pay back, on a shared computer, one button right there: "Remove the wallet from this computer".
