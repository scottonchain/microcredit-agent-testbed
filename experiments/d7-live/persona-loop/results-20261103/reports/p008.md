# Grace, 72, cook — report

## 1. What I wanted
About $3 to top up my prepaid meter. A friend sent the link. I know nothing about crypto and did not want to; I wanted the $3.

## 2. What happened, step by step
1. Opened the link. Yellow band: "not real money. Nothing here is a real loan." So no meter top-up here. (002-look.png)
2. Tapped "Get started". Same page came back; After waiting, a form appeared. (004-wait_5s.png)
3. Looked for "Pay back" before borrowing: not on the form. Pressed back: "Your wallet", "You can borrow up to $0.00". No Pay back. (006-back.png)
4. "More": no Pay back either. "Borrow": "Available $0.00", 9.33% interest. Typed 3: "You have no credit yet." (009)
5. Help says pay within 30 days, not where. (011-tap_Help.png)
6. "Ask for a loan": $3, A bill, In two weeks. "$3.00 now, pay back about $3.03." Clear. Tapped "Ask for this loan". (016)
7. A chat with the "credit officer"; my message was written for me. In 15 seconds: "Your line is set at $3. Tap Borrow." (019-wait_15s.png)
8. "Borrow $3.00 now", "Borrow". "You now owe about $3.00. Due by Mar 13, 2027." Wallet $3.10, and now a "Pay back" button. (023)
9. "Pay back": "You owe right now $3.01... Due in 29 days." "Pay back everything": "Loan closed. You paid $3.01." Wallet $0.10. (024, 025)
10. "Receipt" was only a link. Another website kept "verifying you are not a bot" for half a minute and showed nothing. (028-wait_10s.png)

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1. Front page. I expected a loan; it is practice with play money. Cannot do what I came for. Slowed me (ended my errand).
- F2. Wallet, More, Borrow, Help. I wanted to see how paying back works before borrowing. No "Pay back" button exists until you owe money. Slowed me.
- F3. Wallet and Borrow pages. "Borrow up to $0.00" and a "Borrow" button that goes nowhere until you visit the credit desk. I tapped the wrong one first. Slowed me.
- F4. Front and wallet say "No fees"; Borrow page says "9.33% a year". Interest is a fee to me. Annoyed me.
- F5. "Due in 30 days, by Mar 13, 2027." By my calendar 30 days from today is early December. A minute later: "29 days" and "$3.01" instead of 30 and $3.00. Worried me.
- F6. "You paid $3.01: $3.00 plus less than a cent of interest." A cent is not less than a cent. "$0.00 of that interest counts toward your credit" means nothing to me. Tiny.
- F7. Receipt: I expected amount, date, what for. I got a link to a strange website that never finished checking I am not a robot. Annoyed me.
- F8. Unexplained words: "wallet", "line", "Backing", "recovery code", "public on the test network". And $3.10 after borrowing $3, no word on the extra 10 cents. Tiny.

## 4. Did I get the loan? Did I pay it back?
Yes, $3 of test dollars, in about a minute. Paid back $3.01 straight away. The screen says I owe nothing, $0.10 is left, and I may borrow $3.00 again. My meter is still empty.

## 5. Anything that felt unsafe, like real money, or dishonest
- The "not real money" band on every page was honest.
- "What you write here is public" — to whom? I did not like that.
- Amount and days changing while I looked (F5) felt like a meter running.
- The receipt sending me to a website checking I am "not a bot" felt fishy.
- It never asked for my fingerprint. What stops someone picking up my phone and borrowing?

## 6. Three things I would change
1. A "Pay back" button on the wallet page from the start, greyed if nothing is owed.
2. Say the same thing everywhere: "no fees" or "about 3 cents on $3", not both. Make the due date agree with the calendar.
3. A proper receipt in the app: what I paid, when, that I owe nothing. Not a link to a website that does not open.
