# Luis, 31, cleaner: my go at the loan app

## 1. What I wanted
About $5 to top up the prepaid meter, paid back when I had it. I did not want to say what it was for. I was on the work laptop (Windows), not my phone.

## 2. What happened, step by step
1. Opened the link. Big screen looked fine: one card in the middle, a square code on the right (001-look.png).
2. Tapped the big green "Get started". "One moment…", then a form (003-wait_5s.png).
3. Tapped $5 and "In two weeks". "Ask for this loan" stayed grey: it wanted "What is it for?" (005-tap_In_two_weeks.png).
4. Picked "Something else" (closest to "mind your business") and tapped Ask.
5. It wrote a message in my name: "I need $5 for something I need…" I never typed that (007-tap_Ask_for_this_loan.png).
6. Waited 15 seconds. The officer said yes and never asked what for (008-wait_15s.png).
7. Tapped "Borrow $5.00 now". Straight to "Done": owe $5.00, due Oct 22. No "are you sure", no fingerprint I saw (009-tap_Borrow_5_00_now.png).
8. Came back 25 days later: "You owe today $5.04. Due in 5 days." (011-days_25.png).
9. Tapped "Pay back", then "Pay back everything" (012-tap_Pay_back.png).
10. Done: paid $5.03, loan closed, wallet $0.07, "limit is now $5.01" (013-tap_Pay_back_everything.png).
11. Opened "Receipt": a date, "from wallet 0xAa70…4894", "recorded as 0x5ad77a9d…" (014-tap_Receipt.png).
12. In Help, opened "Use this wallet on another phone": a wall of letters, cut off at the bottom of its box (019-tap_Done_testing_.png).

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1. Loan form: would not let me ask without a reason. Had to pick "Something else", and the officer never asked anyway. Annoyed me.
- F2. Credit desk: the app put words in my mouth and sent them. I wanted to see it first. Slowed me.
- F3. Borrow: one tap and I owed money. No confirm, no fingerprint, though page one said my fingerprint is "the key". Tiny for $5; with real money I want a "yes, do it".
- F4. Pay back: said I owe $5.04, then said I paid $5.03. Which is it? Tiny.
- F5. Done box: "$0.01 of that interest counts toward your own credit, so your limit is now $5.01." No idea what that means. Tiny.
- F6. Receipt: "0xAa70…4894" and "0x5ad77a9d…" mean nothing to me. Not proof I can show anyone. Annoyed me.
- F7. Laptop: every line says "phone" while I am on a work PC. The side box said "your computer's fingerprint, face or PIN is the key": so can the next person on this laptop get into my wallet? Nobody said. Worried me.
- F8. Recovery code: wall of letters, "keep it private", last line cut off. Where do I keep that? Annoyed me.
- F9. After paying, the wallet still says the $0.07 "includes the few cents the credit desk put here to cover your interest". Old line. Tiny.

## 4. Did I get the loan? Did I pay it back?
Yes, $5 at once. Yes, paid $5.03 on day 25, five days before the due date (Oct 22, 2027). I owe nothing. It says I can now borrow up to $5.01.

## 5. Anything unsafe, like real money, or dishonest
The yellow banner says practice money on every page: clear, not scary. But it wrote and sent a message in my name; one tap put me in debt with no confirm; and on a shared work computer I cannot tell if the wallet is now tied to it (the only "remove" is hidden in Help under "Done testing?"). Nothing felt dishonest: the interest was 3 cents, as promised.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. Let me ask without a reason, or give me an "I'd rather not say" button. Do not force a reason the officer does not use.
2. Show me the message before it is sent and let me change it. Then a "Yes, borrow $5" step before I owe anything.
3. A receipt in plain words (date, amount, "paid in full"), and say "computer" when I am on one, with one line on removing the wallet from a shared machine.
