# Sofia, 45, farm worker: trying to get a $2 loan

## 1. What I wanted
Two dollars for ingredients for my weekend food sale. I did not want to say what it was for. One button, done.

## 2. What happened, step by step
1. Opened the link. Yellow band: "practice version, not real money". Tapped the big 'Get started' (001-look.png).
2. "What do you need? One moment…", nothing to tap. Waited. The date on top jumped from Nov 3 to Nov 7 (003-wait_5s.png).
3. Form: how much, what for, when. Tapped '$2' and 'In a week', skipped "what for" (005-tap_In_a_week.png).
4. 'Ask for this loan' stayed grey and would not tap.
5. Tapped 'Write to the credit desk in your own words instead' (006).
6. Typed "I need 2 dollars. I will pay it back in a week. What it is for is my business." Tapped 'Send' (008).
7. Waited 15 seconds. Officer: "Happy to help with your business. You can borrow up to $2..." and a green 'Borrow $2.00 now' appeared (009-wait_15s.png).
8. Tapped it. Box: done, I owe $2.00, due Dec 7, 2026. No fingerprint asked that I noticed (010).
9. Wallet: $2.10, limit $2.00, "backed by the credit desk" (011-tap_OK.png).
10. Came back 3 days later: still owed $2.00 "plus less than a cent" (012-days_3.png).
11. Tapped 'Pay back', 'Pay back everything'. "Loan closed... wallet now has $0.10" (014-tap_Pay_back_everything.png).
12. Tapped 'OK', put the phone down.

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1. The form (005). The button went grey with no message about what was missing; I had to guess it was the reason. Slowed me / annoyed me. "Anyone on the test network can read this" made me even less willing to say.
- F2. The credit desk (009). I wrote "what it is for is my business" meaning none of your business. The officer read it as a business: "Happy to help with your business", and gave me the money. Tiny for me, but the officer is not really reading. If the reason matters it should have pushed back; if it does not, why does the form block on it?
- F3. The date (003). After "one moment" the top said Nov 7, four days after I started. No explanation. Annoyed me.
- F4. Pay-back box (014). "You paid $2.00: the $2.00 you borrowed plus less than a cent of interest." Two dollars equals two dollars plus interest? And my wallet dropped from $2.10 to $0.10, which looks like ten cents, not "less than a cent". Probably rounding, but I had to think. Tiny.
- F5. Words I did not know: "wallet", "test tokens", "backed by", "credit limit", "test network". "0.8% a month (9.33% a year)" meant nothing; "about 2 cents on 2 dollars" I understood.

## 4. Did I get the loan?
Yes, $2.00, the first day. Paid it all back 3 days later with one tap. The screen says I owe nothing, nothing is due, and I can borrow $2 again any time.

## 5. Anything that felt unsafe, like real money, or dishonest
- The yellow band on every screen said not real money. It never felt real. Good.
- The desk "put a few cents in my wallet to pay the interest". Free money from the lender felt odd; the screen admitted a real loan would not do that.
- What I type is "public". That is why I would not write anything true.
- No fingerprint, no "are you sure" before $2 came in or went out. Fast, but with real money I would want one.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. When 'Ask for this loan' is grey, say in red what is missing. Do not make me guess.
2. Let me pick "I'd rather not say" for the reason, or drop it, since the officer paid without one anyway.
3. Fix the money sentences: "$2 and a fraction of a cent", and explain why my wallet lost 10 cents, not "less than a cent".
