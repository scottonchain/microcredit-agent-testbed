# Rosa, 56, taxi driver. Windows laptop at work.

## 1. What I wanted
About $2.50 for a phone charger and headset for the taxi, paid back in 3 days. My friend said "first get test dollars on the Receive screen". I was afraid the app takes real money.

## 2. What happened, step by step
1. First page, yellow line on top: "test tokens, not real money". I relax. Looks fine on the big laptop screen, square code on the right (001-look.png).
2. Tap 'Get started'. "One moment…", then a form: "What do you need?" No fingerprint asked, it just worked.
3. I look for "Receive" like my friend said. Not on this page, not after scrolling. I tap 'Help'.
4. Help says "nothing to fetch". Many words, I skip most (005-tap_Help.png).
5. '‹ Back'. Tap 'Other', type '2.50'. A green line appears: "you get $2.50 now... $0.02 at most". Clear.
6. Tap 'Work tools', 'In a week' (no "3 days" button). It shows the sentence sent in my name (010-tap_In_a_week.png).
7. Tap 'Ask for this loan'. Chat screen "Credit desk", "reading your message…". I wait 15 seconds.
8. Officer answers: "we lend in whole dollars, rounded you up: you can borrow up to $3". Then a green box and a button 'Borrow $2.50 now' (012-wait_15s.png).
9. Tap 'Borrow $2.50 now'. A box: "Done. You now owe $2.50, due by Jan 15, 2027" (013-tap_Borrow_2_50_now.png). Wallet shows $2.60.
10. Tap 'OK'. Under 'More' I find 'Receive': a long code 0x9D50… and "You never need to fetch test dollars yourself" (017-tap_Receive.png).
11. I put the laptop away 3 days. Come back: "You owe today $2.50, plus 0.19 of a cent, due in 27 days".
12. Tap 'Pay back', 'Pay back everything'. Box: "Loan closed... wallet now has $0.10. You owe nothing" (021-tap_Pay_back_everything.png).

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1. Form page: I expected a Receive button at the start. It is hidden under 'More' on the wallet page, seen only after borrowing. I had to read a long Help block to learn I do not need it. **Slowed me.**
- F2. Credit desk: officer says "you can borrow up to $3" but the button says 'Borrow $2.50 now'. For a second I thought it changed my loan to $3. It did not. **Annoyed me.**
- F3. Pay-back time: I wanted 3 days; only 'In a week', 'In two weeks', 'In 30 days'. I picked a week, then it said the loan "runs 30 days" anyway. Why ask me? **Tiny.**
- F4. Receive page: a long code "0x9D50c386…" and "address". I do not know what that is or who I would give it to. **Tiny**, since I did not need it.
- F5. Help page: too many words, "Stake", "Backing", "Recovery code". I skipped them. **Tiny.**
- F6. Wallet $2.60 after borrowing $2.50, $0.10 after paying. Explained, but I had to think where the 10 cents came from. **Tiny.**

## 4. Did I get the loan? Did I pay it back?
Yes, I got $2.50. Yes, I paid it all back after 3 days, with less than one cent of interest. The screen says I owe nothing. Nothing is due. I could borrow again up to $3.

## 5. Anything unsafe, like real money, or dishonest
Nothing felt like real money: the yellow banner stayed on every screen. Interest said "$0.02 at most" and was 0.19 of a cent: honest. "Public on the test network" made me careful, but it told me. The long 0x codes look like bank things I do not understand, a little scary, but it never asked for a card or password.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. Put 'Receive' (or a line "you do not need to get test dollars, the desk gives them") on the very first page, so friends stop telling each other to fetch dollars.
2. Make the officer's answer and the button say the same number: "You asked $2.50, approved. You may take up to $3." in one line.
3. Let me pick my own pay-back day (3 days), or drop the question and just say "pay back any time within 30 days".
