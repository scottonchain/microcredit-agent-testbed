# Olu, 38, cleaner. Persona 17.

## 1. What I wanted
My meter is low. I wanted about $8 to top it up and pay it back on the last day.

## 2. What happened, step by step
1. First screen: "Try a small loan", "up to $5", "test dollars, not real money" (001-look.png). I need $8. Tapped Get started anyway.
2. "One moment…", then a form: how much, what for, when pay back (003-wait_5s.png).
3. Tapped Other, typed "8k" by mistake. Orange box: "Between $1 and $5 in this practice version, like 3.75." (007-tap_In_30_days.png)
4. Fixed it to "8". Same message. No $8 for me.
5. Tapped $5, A bill, In 30 days, Ask for this loan.
6. Credit desk chat wrote my message for me. Waited 15 s. Officer said yes, $5, "a few cents in your wallet for the interest" (011-wait_15s.png).
7. Tapped Borrow $5.00 now. Fingerprint just worked. Box: "You now owe $5.00, due by Oct 22, 2027" (012-tap_Borrow_5_00_now.png).
8. Wallet: $5.10, "Due in 30 days". A moment later, same screen: "You owe today $5.04, Due in 5 days" (014-scroll_down.png). I touched nothing.
9. Tapped Help. Long text. Went back.
10. Came back 4 days later: "Due in 1 day". One more day: due today.
11. Tapped Pay back: "You owe right now $5.04, due today, pay today and it is on time" (021-tap_Pay_back.png). Tapped Pay back everything.
12. Box: "Loan closed. You paid $5.04", wallet $0.06, limit now $5.02 (022-tap_Pay_back_everything.png).

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1. First screen. I expected a loan for my meter. I saw "up to $5", "not real money, nothing to buy with it", in small text. So this cannot pay my meter. Blocked me (for my real need).
- F2. Amount box. I typed "8k". The box took the letter. The message said "between $1 and $5", not "letters not allowed". After fixing to 8 the message was the same. I read it twice to understand. Slowed me.
- F3. Wallet after borrowing. The box said "due in 30 days". Then the wallet said "Due in 5 days", "owe today $5.04", by itself. I got a fright. The 5 days were real: 4 days later it said 1 day. Where did my 25 days go? Scared me / slowed me.
- F4. Credit desk. Before Borrow nobody said in one line "you get $5, you pay $5.04 on Oct 22". I saw the total only after, on the wallet. "Interest" I did not understand at first. Annoyed me.
- F5. Pay back box. "3.83 cents of interest", "$0.02 counts toward your own credit, limit now $5.02". What is 3.83 cents? Why does my limit grow 2 cents? Tiny.
- F6. Words I did not know: wallet, test network, backing, vouches, credit limit, stake, recovery code. Help explains them in one long block I skipped. Tiny.

## 4. Did I get the loan? Did I pay it back?
I got $5, not $8. I paid $5.04 on the due day, Oct 22, 2027, on time. The screen says I owe nothing. $0.06 play money is left. I can borrow $5.02 again.

## 5. Anything unsafe, like real money, or dishonest
- The yellow banner said "not real money" on every screen, so it never felt like real money. Good. But then why did my friend send me this for my meter?
- "What you write is public on the test network" felt a bit exposed.
- My fingerprint worked for Borrow and Pay back and I never saw what it opened. Like signing without reading.
- 30 days turning into 5 days looked like a trick.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. At the very top, big: "Practice only. You cannot pay a bill with this." Or let me ask for the amount I really need.
2. Amount box: numbers only, and say "8 is more than 5, the most here" in plain words.
3. Before Borrow, one line: "You get $5.00 today. You pay $5.04 by Oct 22." And keep the days honest: no jump from 30 to 5 on its own.
