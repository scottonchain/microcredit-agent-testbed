# Ahmed, 24, student — report

## 1. What I wanted
About $2.50 for a school uniform, and to pay it back after 10 days. My friend sent me the link. I know nothing about crypto.

## 2. What happened, step by step
- Opened the link. "Your wallet $0.00", "You can borrow up to $0.00". Yellow "test network" banner on top. (004-look.png)
- Tapped Borrow. "Available to borrow $0.00", Borrow button grey. (005-tap_Borrow.png)
- Typed 2.50 anyway. Orange box: "You have no credit yet. Ask the credit desk". (006-type_How_much_.png)
- Went back, tapped "Ask the credit desk". A chat with an "AI credit officer". (008-tap_Ask_the_credit_desk.png)
- Wrote: student, 2.50 for a uniform, pay in 10 days. Tapped Send. A box asked for my fingerprint just to send a chat. (010-tap_Send_to_the_desk.png)
- Waited 15 s. The desk answered: it gives me a $3 line, "You can tap Borrow now". (011-wait_15s.png)
- Tapped OK, then Borrow. Now "Available to borrow $3.00". (013-tap_Borrow.png)
- Typed 2.50, tapped Borrow. Button went grey, nothing else. (015-tap_Borrow.png)
- Waited 5 s. "Borrow $2.50 went through. You now owe about $2.50. Due 12/3/2026." (016-wait_5s.png)
- Home: wallet $2.60, "You owe $2.50, due in 30 days". (018-tap_Back.png)
- Came back 10 days later: "You owe $2.51, due in 20 days". (019-days_10.png)
- Tapped Pay back: "You owe right now $2.51", big "Pay back everything" button. (020-tap_Pay_back.png)
- Tapped it. "Loan closed. You paid $2.51". (021-tap_Pay_back_everything.png)
- Home: wallet $0.09, "borrow up to $3.00", Pay back grey. (023-tap_Back.png)
- Tapped Help: 4 steps, then a huge code and a "Remove this wallet" button. (025-tap_Help.png)

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1. Home/Borrow screens: I expected to type 2.50 and get it. Instead the big number said $0.00 and I had to find a chat with a "credit desk" first. Slowed me.
- F2. Sending the chat message: the phone asked for my fingerprint just to send text. I expected that only for money. Felt like signing something I had not read. Annoyed me.
- F3. Words nobody explained: "USDC", "faucet", "0x2839…9c43", "Backed by 0x8E2B…e41b", "block explorer". Help says "You get test USDC from a faucet" and never explains either word. Annoyed me.
- F4. After borrowing $2.50 my wallet said $2.60. Nobody said where the extra 10 cents came from. Tiny, but strange for a money app.
- F5. After tapping Borrow the button only went grey, no "sending..." message. I was not sure I had tapped it. Slowed me.
- F6. The pay-back "Done" box said "$0.00 of that interest counts toward your own credit". I have no idea what that means. Tiny.
- F7. Help page shows a giant code "eyJpZCI6..." and a "Remove this wallet from this phone" button next to the help text. I was scared to tap anything there. Annoyed me.

## 4. Did I get the loan? Did I pay it back?
Yes. I got $2.50 straight away, after the desk chat. After 10 days I paid $2.51 (about 1 cent interest). The screen says the loan is closed and I owe nothing. It says I can borrow up to $3.00 again.

## 5. Anything unsafe, like real money, or dishonest
- The yellow banner said everything is test money, so it never felt like real money.
- Fingerprint for a chat message felt like a contract.
- The chat said "Everything written here is public", and I had to write what I need the money for. Uncomfortable.
- The Help page shows what looks like a secret password in the open, with a "Remove this wallet" button right there.
- Nothing felt dishonest; the numbers added up.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. Let me type the amount on the first screen and have the app ask the desk for me, instead of sending me to a chat.
2. Say "test dollars" everywhere and drop "USDC", "faucet" and the 0x codes, or explain them in one line where they appear.
3. Show "Sending..." after I tap Borrow, and do not ask for my fingerprint for a chat message (or tell me why).
