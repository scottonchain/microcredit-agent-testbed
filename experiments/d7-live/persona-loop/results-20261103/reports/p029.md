# Tom, 41, retired teacher. Phone in dark mode, cracked screen.

## 1. What I wanted
About $2 to top up my prepaid meter, paid back the next day. A friend sent the link.

## 2. What happened, step by step
1. Opened the link. Yellow banner: "test tokens, not real money". No meter top-up, then. Read on anyway. (001-look.png)
2. Tapped 'Get started'. "One moment…", nothing to tap. Waited 5 seconds. (002)
3. "What do you need?" page: my wallet was ready and opens with my fingerprint. Nobody asked for one. (003)
4. Tapped '$2', 'A bill', 'In a week' (no "tomorrow" choice). A green box told me: get $2.00 now, about 1 cent interest in a week, 2 cents at most, no other fees. Clear. (006-tap_In_a_week.png)
5. Tapped 'Ask for this loan'. A chat with "the credit officer (an AI)" opened; my sentence was sent for me. (007)
6. Waited 15 seconds. The officer said yes, "The desk vouches for you", and that it put a few cents in my wallet to cover interest. (008-wait_15s.png)
7. Tapped 'Borrow $2.00 now'. Box: "done", I owe $2.00, due Dec 6, 2026. No fingerprint prompt that I noticed. (009)
8. Tapped 'OK'. Wallet shows $2.10. "You are backed by" line in typewriter letters, "vou-ches" split, "$2.00" on two lines. (010-tap_OK.png)
9. Came back the next day. "You owe today $2.00 ... plus 0.05 of a cent of interest". (011)
10. Tapped 'Pay back', then 'Pay back everything'. Box: loan closed, paid $2.00, wallet now $0.10. (013-tap_Pay_back_everything.png)
11. Opened 'Receipt': "from wallet 0x02A7…0706 ... recorded as 0x3cf001ce…". Gibberish to me. (014)
12. Tapped 'Tell us how it went', typed a note, 'Send'. "Your note is with the team." Done.

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1. First screen: I expected a loan for my meter; I saw "not real money, you cannot pay a bill". Honest, but it ended my real purpose before I started. Severity: blocked my real goal (not the test).
- F2. "What do you need?" screen said my wallet "opens with your fingerprint", but I was never asked for one, not then and not when borrowing. I do not know whether anything is locked. Severity: annoyed / worried me.
- F3. Chat: "The desk vouches for you", "Backing is a promise to cover your loan". I do not know who the desk is or why a machine vouches for me. Severity: tiny.
- F4. Wallet screen: "backed by" line in a typewriter font, "vou ches" split, amount broken as "$2." / "00". On a cracked screen it looks like a glitch. Severity: annoyed me.
- F5. Receipt: long code strings instead of plain words. Severity: tiny.
- F6. "0.05 of a cent of interest": odd wording; I read it twice. Severity: tiny.
- F7. The calendar jumped from Nov 3 to Nov 6 right after 'Get started'. Severity: tiny.
- F8. No "tomorrow" option for paying back; nearest was "In a week". Severity: tiny.

## 4. Did I get the loan? Did I pay it back?
Yes, $2.00 of test dollars, in a couple of minutes. Paid back the next day with one tap. The screen says I owe nothing, nothing is due, $0.10 of test dollars left. Original due date was Dec 6, 2026.

## 5. Anything that felt unsafe, like real money, or dishonest
Nothing felt like real money; the banner said so on every screen, which was honest. Two things nagged: my message "is public on the test network", and the fingerprint promised but never asked for. The "AI officer" deciding in 15 seconds felt like a slot machine, not a decision.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. Tell me at the very first line that this cannot pay a real bill, before the big "Try a small loan" title, so people like me with a meter to feed do not get their hopes up.
2. Fix the "backed by" line: normal letters, one line, amount not split.
3. Make the receipt say "Loan of $2.00 paid in full on Nov 7" in plain words, and hide the code strings behind "details".
