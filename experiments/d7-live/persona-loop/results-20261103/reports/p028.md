# Bella, 72, shop assistant

## 1. What I wanted
About $8 for a tool for a job on Friday. A friend sent me the link and said it was a loan app.

## 2. What happened, step by step
- Opened the link. Yellow stripe: "Practice version ... not real money." I thought maybe that was just the first go (001-look.png).
- Tapped 'Get started'. Fingerprint, no forms, no password. Nice.
- A page "What do you need?" with buttons $1 $2 $3 $5 and 'Other'. Tapped 'Other', typed '8'. It told me "$8 is more than the $5 maximum" (006-type_e_g_3_75.png).
- Gave in: '$5', 'Work tools', 'In a week'. Green box: about $0.01 extra in a week (009-tap_In_a_week.png).
- Tapped 'Ask for this loan'. A chat with an AI "credit officer". About 15 seconds; it said yes (011-wait_15s.png).
- Tapped 'Borrow $5.00 now'. A box said "Done. You now owe $5.00, due by Dec 17, 2026" (012-tap_Borrow_5_00_now.png).
- Wallet shows $5.10. Looked for a way to get it to the shop. Opened 'More', tapped 'Send money'. It wants "your friend's address ... starts with 0x". No idea what that is (015-tap_Send_money.png).
- Tapped 'Help'. Words like Stake and Recovery code; test dollars "have no value". Nothing about my shop (016-tap_Help.png).
- Went back to the credit desk and asked how to get the money out for the tool. The officer told me to "tap Borrow" and the dollars are "yours to spend on your tool" (022-wait_15s.png). But I had already borrowed and the Borrow button was grey.
- Asked again. This time it said plainly they cannot go to a shop or a bank (025-wait_15s.png).
- Tapped 'Pay back', 'Pay back everything'. "Loan closed ... plus 0 of a cent of interest. Your wallet now has $0.10" (028-tap_Pay_back_everything.png).
- Put the phone down.

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1. Front page. I expected a loan I could use. It said practice, but my friend said loan, so I kept going hoping real money came later. It never does. Blocked me.
- F2. Credit desk. I asked how to get the money to the shop; the officer said "tap Borrow" and spend it on my tool. I had already borrowed, and the dollars cannot be spent. Wrong twice in one answer; owned up only when I asked again. Slowed me and I stopped trusting it.
- F3. 'Send money' screen. "Address that starts with 0x" means nothing to me. There is no "send to my bank" and no "pay a shop". Blocked me.
- F4. The $8 I needed. Buttons stop at $5 and 'Other' refused me. Annoyed me.
- F5. Help page. "Stake", "Recovery code", "test network" made me nervous, and it did not answer my one question. Slowed me.
- F6. Pay-back box said "plus 0 of a cent of interest". Odd English; I had to read it twice. Tiny.
- F7. On the loan page the phone's date read two weeks later than when I opened the app. Tiny, but unsettling.

## 4. Did I get the loan?
I got $5 of pretend dollars, not the $8 I needed and not real. I paid it all back the same day. I owe nothing. Nothing is due. I still do not have my tool.

## 5. Anything unsafe or dishonest
Nothing felt like real money being taken; it kept saying practice. But the officer saying the dollars were "yours to spend on your tool" was not honest, even by mistake; a less careful person would turn up at the shop on Friday with nothing. My fingerprint was used before I understood what for, which made me uneasy.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. Say on the very first screen, in plain words: "You cannot buy anything with this. If you need real money, this is not it yet." Before I press anything.
2. Make the credit officer check what I already did before answering, and never say I can spend the dollars.
3. Show the $5 limit before I start, and let me say what I need; then tell me honestly whether it is enough.
