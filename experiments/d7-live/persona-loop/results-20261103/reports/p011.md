# Omar, 45, nurse's aide. Report on the loan app.

## 1. What I wanted
About $8 for the deposit on a rented sewing machine. Borrow it, pay some now, the rest when I get paid.

## 2. What happened, step by step
- Front page: "Try a small loan", "up to $5 of test dollars" (002-look.png). Tapped the big green Get started.
- A quiz: how much ($1 to $5), what for, when (004-wait_5s.png). Tapped "Ask for this loan" first: greyed, nothing happened.
- Picked $5, Work tools, In two weeks (007-tap_In_two_weeks.png).
- Tapped Ask for this loan. "Done" box, behind it a chat with a "credit officer (an AI)" (008-tap_Ask_for_this_loan.png).
- Waited 15 s. Officer said yes, "a few cents were added to your wallet to cover the interest" (010-wait_15s.png).
- Went Back to the wallet screen looking for Pay back. Not there; a line says it "appears here" when you owe (012-tap_Back.png). Tapped the words anyway: nothing.
- Tapped Borrow. Typed 8: "more than you can borrow right now ($5.00)" (014). Typed 5, Borrow.
- Done: "You now owe $5.00, due by May 27, 2027." Wallet $5.10 (016-tap_Borrow.png).
- Pay back > Pay only part > 2 > Pay this part. "Paid $2.00. Still owed: $3.00" (021-tap_Pay_this_part.png).
- Wallet screen still said "about $5.04 by the due date" (022-tap_OK.png). Put the phone away.
- 10 days later: owe $3.02, due in 20 days (023-days_10.png).
- Pay back > Pay back everything. "Loan closed. You paid $3.01 now and $5.01 in total" (025-tap_Pay_back_everything.png).
- Receipt: "Nov 3, 2026, 2:04 PM ... wallet 0xdF3d…23B2 ... 0xcaccb00b…" (026-tap_Receipt.png).

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1. First screen and quiz: I needed $8, there was only up to $5 and no box for my own number; on the Borrow screen I typed 8 and it said too much. And "test dollars", "nothing to buy with it": this cannot pay my deposit at all. My friend said it was a loan app. Slowed me / annoyed me.
- F2. Wallet screen before the loan: no Pay back button, only a sentence that it appears later. Tiny, the sentence saved it.
- F3. Wallet screen after paying $2: "about $5.04 by the due date" stayed while I owed $3.00, and ten days later still $5.04 while I owed $3.02. I doubted which number was true. Annoyed me.
- F4. Closing box: owed $3.02, "paid $3.01", "$5.01 in total: $5.00 plus about $0.02". Pennies, but 5.00 + 0.02 is not 5.01. Annoyed me.
- F5. Receipt: date Nov 3, 2026 when the phone said May 2027; "wallet 0xdF3d…23B2", "0xcaccb00b…", "public record site, may check you are not a robot". No idea what that is. Tiny.
- F6. Quiz: the big button was grey and silent when I tapped it first. Tiny.
- F7. "Who vouches for you", "backed by The credit desk", "public on the test network". I asked nobody to vouch and do not know what a test network is or who reads it. Tiny.
- F8. After paying, my limit became $5.01. Tiny.

## 4. Did I get the loan?
Yes, $5 (not the $8 I needed). Paid $2 the same day and $3.01 ten days later. Screen says loan closed, I owe nothing, wallet $0.09, I can borrow $5.01 again.

## 5. Anything unsafe, real money, dishonest
The yellow banner on every screen says practice, not real money, so nothing felt like real money. Fingerprint just worked. Odd: the desk giving me cents to pay its own interest; the receipt date from the wrong year; warnings that what I write is public. Not dishonest, but nobody says who is lending the money.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. Say on the first screen, in big letters, that this money cannot pay for anything, and let me type the amount I need, with the cap next to the box.
2. After I pay part, change the "by the due date" number to what I will really owe.
3. Make the receipt plain: today's date, "paid $3.01 on May 7", no 0x codes, pennies that add up.
