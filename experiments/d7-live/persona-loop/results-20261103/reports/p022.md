# Priya, 24, hairdresser: a small loan for a child's medicine

## 1. What I wanted
My little one needs medicine, about $6. A friend sent me a link for small loans. I asked for $20 to be safe, to pay back the next day.

## 2. What happened, step by step
- Opened the link: "Try a small loan. Borrow up to $5 of test dollars." A yellow strip says practice money, not real (001-look.png).
- Tapped "Get started". "One moment…" with nothing to tap, then a form (003-wait_5s.png).
- Amounts went $1 to $5 plus Other. Tapped Other, typed 20. Orange box: "$20 is more than the $5 maximum" (005-type_e_g_3_75.png).
- Tapped "Write to the credit desk in your own words", asked for $20 for my child's medicine, paying back tomorrow.
- Tapped Send. My message vanished, nothing said "sending" (008-tap_Send.png). About 10 seconds later the officer answered kindly: limit is $5 (009-wait_10s.png).
- Tapped "Borrow $5.00 now". At once: "Done. You now owe $5.00, due by Dec 3, 2026." Wallet $5.10 (010-tap_Borrow_5_00_now.png).
- Looked for a way to get the money out: "Send money" wants "your friend's address, it starts with 0x" (013-tap_Send_money.png). Gave up.
- Next day: "You owe today $5.01" (015-days_1.png). Tapped Pay back, then "Pay back everything" (016-tap_Pay_back.png).
- "Loan closed. You paid $5.00 … plus 0.13 of a cent. Wallet $0.10" (017-tap_Pay_back_everything.png). Receipt: codes like 0x86d9…C7Dc, no amount (018-tap_Receipt.png).
- Read Help (020-tap_Help.png). "Done testing?" offers to remove the wallet, "save the code above first". Did not dare (021-tap_Done_testing_.png).

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1. First screen: I expected money for medicine. It says "nothing to buy with it". Honest, but for my real need: blocked me.
- F2. Amount: refused $20 twice (form, then desk). Annoyed me; the reason was clear.
- F3. Credit desk after Send: my words vanished, no "sending…" or tick. I nearly typed it again. Slowed me.
- F4. Officer said "put a few cents in your wallet". I never made a wallet; it just existed. Tiny.
- F5. "Borrow $5.00 now" made the loan on one tap, no "are you sure", no fingerprint I noticed. Felt sudden. Annoyed me.
- F6. Getting money out: "address that starts with 0x" means nothing to me; a pharmacy has no such thing. Blocked me for the real need.
- F7. Pay back: "$5.01" at the top, "0.13 cents of interest" below, then "you paid $5.00". Did I pay $5.00 or $5.01? Annoyed me.
- F8. "The approval is a cent or two higher": what is an approval? Tiny.
- F9. Receipt: two long codes, no amount, and a link that "may check you are not a robot". Did not tap it. Annoyed me.
- F10. "Done testing?": "save the code above first". Which code? Never saw one. Tiny.

## 4. Did I get the loan? Did I pay it back?
Yes, $5 (not $20, not the $6 I needed). Paid back the next day. Screen says I owe nothing and can borrow again; $0.10 of test dollars left. Due date would have been Dec 3, 2026.

## 5. Anything unsafe, like real money, or dishonest
Nothing dishonest: the yellow strip and the officer were clear it is practice money and $5 is the limit. Nervous moments: being told what I write is public (I still wrote "medicine for my child"); the loan happening on one tap; the 0x codes and the "public record site" link, which looked like something not to touch; the "remove this wallet" button with a code I never saw.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. On the first screen, say in big words that this money cannot buy anything, or how I would ever get real money out. I came with a sick child and wasted hope.
2. After Send, show my message at once with "sending…"; before the loan, show "Are you sure? You will owe about $5.04 by Dec 3" with a Yes button.
3. One number on the pay-back screen ($5.01 or $5.00, not both), and a receipt that says the amount and date in plain words instead of 0x codes.
