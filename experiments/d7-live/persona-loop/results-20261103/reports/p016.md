# Elena, 45, cook. Persona 16, run 4217.

## 1. What I wanted
About $2 for a small tool for a job on Friday. Borrow it, pay it back, and borrow again straight after.

## 2. What happened, step by step
1. Opened the link. Yellow banner: practice version, not real money, "nothing to buy with it" (001-look.png). Not what I came for, but I tried it.
2. Tapped Get started. "One moment…", then a form "What do you need?" (004-wait_5s.png). No fingerprint was asked.
3. Tapped $2, Work tools, In a week. Green box: $2.00 now, about $0.01 interest in a week, $0.02 at most, no other fees (007-tap_In_a_week.png). Clear.
4. Tapped Ask for this loan. It became a chat with a "credit desk" (008).
5. Waited 15 seconds. The officer (an AI) agreed, said it put "a few cents" in my wallet for the interest (009-wait_15s.png).
6. Tapped Borrow $2.00 now. "Done. You now owe $2.00, due by Oct 22, 2027." Receipt: "from wallet 0x2173…4bD8", "recorded on the test network as 0x0f2c4cf3…" (011-tap_Receipt.png).
7. Tapped OK. Wallet: $2.10, limit $2.00, "backed by The credit desk $2.00", and "You owe today $2.02. Due in 5 days" (012-tap_OK.png). One tap earlier it was $2.00 and 30 days.
8. Tapped Pay back, Pay back everything (014). "Loan closed. You paid $2.01: $2.00 plus 1.28 cents of interest. Wallet $0.09" (015-tap_Pay_back_everything.png).
9. Tapped Borrow, typed 2, tapped Borrow (018, 019). "Done. You now owe $2.00, due by Nov 21, 2027." Wallet $2.09 (020-tap_Borrow.png). No questions this time.
10. Opened More and Help (023-tap_Help.png). "Done testing?" says "save the code above": a block of about 200 letters (025-tap_Use_this_wallet_on_another.png). Left it alone.

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1. Wallet screen after the first loan (012). Expected "$2.00, due in 30 days", as the Done box had just said. Saw "$2.02, due in 5 days". I had only tapped OK. I checked three times. My phone said November 2026 when I started; the app talks about October 2027. Slowed me, and I stopped trusting the numbers.
- F2. First page. I came for a real tool. It says the dollars are practice and never says whether a real version exists. For my real need: blocked me. The practice itself worked.
- F3. Pay back. The screen said I owe $2.02; the Done box said I paid $2.01 with "1.28 cents" of interest. Which is right, and what is 0.28 of a cent? Tiny.
- F4. Receipts. "0x2173…4bD8", "0x0f2c4cf3…", "public record site, may check you are not a robot". No idea what these are or why I need them. Annoyed me.
- F5. Second loan: typed 2 and had it, no officer, no "what for". I wondered if I had skipped a step. Tiny.
- F6. Help, "Done testing?": "save the code above". Nobody can write down 200 letters, and it does not say what happens to my $2 debt if I lose the phone. Annoyed me.
- F7. "The approval is a cent or two higher": I never approved anything. And the fingerprint never came up: is the wallet locked at all? Tiny.

## 4. Did I get the loan?
Yes, $2.00 of test dollars, twice. Paid the first back: $2.01. I now owe $2.00 on the second, due Nov 21, 2027 by the screen, about $2.02 if I wait until then. Wallet shows $2.09.

## 5. Anything unsafe, like real money, or dishonest
The yellow banner is on every screen, so I never took it for real money. Still, "anyone on the test network can read this" about why I borrow, someone "vouching" for me, and the jump from 30 to 5 days felt like real-money worries. Nothing felt dishonest.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. Say on the first screen whether a real version exists and when.
2. Never let the due date or amount change on its own while I am looking. If time has moved on, say "today is Oct 17".
3. Plain words on receipts, a short recovery code, and one line about what happens to my debt if I lose the phone.
