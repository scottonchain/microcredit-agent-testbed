# Yara, 56, retired teacher: my report

## 1. What I wanted
About $3.75 for a bus pass until payday. A friend sent the link. I also wanted a phone number or a person in case something went wrong, and when I came back late I wanted to put it right.

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1. Credit desk (009, 013, 031). Three times "$4 now", each time with "nothing left, try later". Button and wallet never passed $2.01. I wanted one straight answer. Blocked me: never enough for the bus pass, and I stopped believing it.
- F2. Credit desk. "Free stake": no idea what that is; Help does not say. Annoyed me.
- F3. Everywhere. No phone number, no person. For money I want a person. Slowed and worried me.
- F4. Credit desk (031). Officer said twice "I've put a few cents in your wallet"; wallet stayed $0.08. Annoyed me, less trust.
- F5. Wallet (022). Red box says late, then "about $2.02 by the due date if you wait", but the date had passed. Tiny.
- F6. Pay back (023). "The approval is a cent or two higher": approval of what? Feared more would be taken. Tiny.
- F7. Every chat message pops a "Done" box to OK before I see the reply. Annoyed me.
- F8. "Your address" is a jumble, 0x958d50... I don't know what to do with it. Slowed me.

## 4. Did I get the loan?
$2, not $3.75. Paid back 5 days late, $2.02 in all. The screen says I owe nothing and can borrow $2.01. No due date now. Being late cost nothing, they said.

## 5. Unsafe, real money, dishonest
- Nothing felt like real money: the banner is on every screen.
- Dishonest-feeling: promising $4 three times when the app only had $2.01; "I've put a few cents in" with nothing arriving; sorry, then the same again.
- "What you write is public": glad they warned, odd that my bus-pass request is public.
- The fingerprint "just worked" without my seeing it asked; I never knew when.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. Let the officer promise only what the button can do: "$2 today", full stop. Never "$4 now" and "try later" in one breath.
2. Give me a person or a phone number, and explain "stake" in Help or drop the word.
3. Drop the "Done" pop-up on chat; on the red late box, say what being late means (no fee, pay as soon as you can).
