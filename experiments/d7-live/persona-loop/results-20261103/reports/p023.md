# Kwame, 22, home carer: report

## 1. What I wanted
A small loan, about $8, to top up my prepaid meter, paid back after 10 days. A friend sent me the link.

## 2. What happened, step by step
1. Opened the link. Yellow banner: "Practice version... not real money." Page: "Try a small loan", up to $5 (001-look.png).
2. Tapped Get started. "One moment…", then a form "What do you need?" (003-wait_5s.png).
3. Tapped Other, typed 8: "$8 is more than the $5 maximum" (005-type_e_g_3_75.png).
4. Tapped $5, A bill, In two weeks. It said about $0.02 interest, $0.04 at most, no other fees, and showed the message it would send (008-tap_In_two_weeks.png).
5. Tapped Ask for this loan. A "Credit desk" chat opened. Waited 15 seconds; the officer agreed and said it put a few cents in my wallet for the interest (010-wait_15s.png).
6. Tapped Borrow $5.00 now. Straight to "Done": owe $5.00, due Dec 3, 2026. Wallet $5.10 (011-tap_Borrow_5_00_now.png).
7. Wallet: "You owe today $5.00", green Pay back button (013-scroll_down.png).
8. Phone away 10 days. Nov 13: "You owe today $5.02" (014-days_10.png).
9. Tapped Pay back: "You owe right now $5.02", wallet $5.10 (015-tap_Pay_back.png).
10. Tapped Pay back everything. "Done. Loan closed. You paid $5.01." Wallet $0.09 (016-tap_Pay_back_everything.png).
11. Opened Receipt: "from wallet 0xE5Eb…8A10 ... 0xb2ce4fe9…" (017-tap_Receipt.png).
12. Opened More: Send money, Receive, Back a friend. No "tell us how it went" (019-tap_More.png). Put the phone down.

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1. First page. Expected a loan app for my meter. Saw "test dollars, not real money, nothing to buy with it" and a $5 maximum. My real problem ($8) was not solved. Honest, but the link was a dead end for me. Blocked me (for my real need); the practice itself was easy.
- F2. First page. "Wallet", "recovery code", "home-screen app", "restore": I did not know what a wallet is here, I thought of my bank. Ignored the box, pressed Get started. Slowed me a little.
- F3. Pay back. It said I owe $5.02, then Done said I paid $5.01. Which is it? With money I want one number. And "The approval is a cent or two higher": I never approved anything, what approval? Annoyed me.
- F4. Receipt. "0xE5Eb…8A10" means nothing to me; my bank gives a reference I can read out. Tiny.
- F5. After paying. First page said "tell us how it went" but there is no button for it, not under More either. Gave up. Annoyed me.
- F6. Borrow. "Borrow $5.00 now" went straight to Done, no "are you sure?" like my bank app. Too quick for borrowing. Tiny.
- F7. Wallet. "Who vouches for you: The credit desk $5.00", "Backing". I never asked anyone to vouch for me; did not understand why it is there. Tiny.
- I never needed Help.

## 4. Did I get the loan? Did I pay it back?
Yes, $5 (not the $8 I needed) on Nov 3. Paid back $5.01 on Nov 13. The screen says I owe nothing, $0.09 is left, and I can borrow up to $5.01 again. Due date was Dec 3, so I was early.

## 5. Anything unsafe, like real money, or dishonest
Nothing felt like real money: the yellow banner was on every screen. The warning that my message is public made me careful; good. Two odd things: the "credit officer" answered in seconds, so I doubt it read anything; and a lender putting cents in my wallet to pay its own interest would look like a trick in real life, even though it was explained.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. Say on the very first line: practice only, max $5, and where to go for a real loan. Not after I typed 8.
2. One number: if it says I owe $5.02, take $5.02, or say $5.01 from the start. Drop the "approval" sentence.
3. After I pay back, a "Tell us how it went" button, and a plain reference number on the receipt instead of 0x letters.
