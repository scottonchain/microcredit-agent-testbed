# Sam's report (61, home carer)

## 1. What I wanted
About $3.75 to pay a late electricity bill, paid back on the last day when I get paid. My friend sent me the link.

## 2. What happened, step by step
- Opened the link. Yellow banner: "practice version, not real money", so it cannot pay my bill. Went on anyway (002-look.png).
- Get started. "How much?" had only $1, $2, $3, $5 buttons, no box for $3.75 (004-wait_5s.png).
- Tapped "Write to the credit desk", typed "$3,75 ... late electricity bill ... 30 days", Send.
- 15 seconds later the officer read it as $3.75, offered "up to $4", and said cents for the interest were in my wallet (010-wait_15s.png).
- Tapped "Borrow $4.00 now". Done at once, no "are you sure". Owe $4.00, due Apr 28, 2027 (011-tap_Borrow_4_00_now.png).
- Came back 29 days later: "Due today. Pay back today and it is on time", owe $4.03 (014-days_29.png).
- Pay back: "You are $0.04 short. The interest is not in your wallet yet. Get test USDC from the faucet" (015-tap_Pay_back.png).
- Open the faucet: a strange page, "Testnet Faucet", Network, Token, Wallet address 0x... (016-tap_Open_the_faucet.png).
- Copy my address: nothing happened; paste into the faucet did nothing (018-tap_Copy_my_address.png).
- Found the address under More, Receive, with "choose Base Sepolia and USDC" (027-tap_Receive.png). Typed 42 characters by hand; Get tokens: "Sent 20 USDC".
- Back in the app: "Late by 9 days", then "Late by 39 days ... counts as a default" (035-tap_Pay_back.png). I never put the phone down.
- Thumb hit "Pay only part". Typed "$4,o8" (a letter o by mistake), then "$4,08": "Pay this part" did nothing, no message (038, 041).
- "Pay back everything": "Loan closed. You paid $4.07" (042-tap_Pay_back_everything.png).

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1. How much? Expected to type $3.75. Four round buttons only; the desk rounded me up to $4.00 with no way to take less. Annoyed me.
- F2. "Borrow $4.00 now". Expected a check question. One tap and I owed money. Felt risky.
- F3. Pay back on the due day. The app had said twice the interest cents were in my wallet. Then: 4 cents short, interest "not in your wallet yet". Blocked me, and made me late.
- F4. The faucet: Testnet, USDC, Network, Ethereum Sepolia, wallet address, none explained. The Pay back screen did not say which network; only the Receive page did. Blocked me.
- F5. Copy gave no "copied", paste did nothing, the address was not on the Pay back screen. 42 letters by hand on a cracked screen. Blocked me.
- F6. The date ran away while I was on the faucet page: due today, then 9 days late, then 39 and "default". A note blamed a clock shared with other testers, but the app itself scolded me. Frightened me.
- F7. Pay only part: "$4,o8" and "$4,08" got no reply at all. Annoyed me.
- F8. Receipt: "paid $4.07: $4.00 plus about $0.08 interest" adds to $4.08. Tiny.

## 4. Did I get the loan? Pay it back?
Yes: $4.00, not the $3.75 I asked for. Paid $4.07, which the app called 39 days late. I owe nothing now; limit $4.03; $19.93 of test dollars in the wallet. My real bill is still unpaid.

## 5. Unsafe, real money, dishonest?
The banner was honest. But another company's page wanting a "wallet address" is what I am warned about. "Default" and "the desk would lose what they put up" felt like real debt. The promise that the interest was in my wallet was not kept. Everything I wrote is public, including that I am a carer with a late bill.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. Let me type $3.75 and lend me that, not $4.
2. Keep the promise about the interest cents. If I must use a faucet, put my address, a Copy that says "Copied", and the network name on the Pay back screen.
3. Ask "Are you sure?" before the loan is made, and make Pay this part say what is wrong with my typing instead of doing nothing.
