# Daniel, 24, shop assistant: trying the loan app

## 1. What I wanted
About $1 for ingredients for a weekend food sale, paid back the next day. My friend sent the link and said "go to the Receive screen first and get test dollars".

## 2. What happened, step by step
- Opened the link. Yellow banner: practice, not real money. Tapped 'Get started' (001-look.png).
- "One moment…", then a form "What do you need?" saying my wallet was ready. No fingerprint prompt appeared; it just worked (003-wait_5s.png).
- Looked for "Receive" as told. Not there. Scrolled, nothing.
- Tapped 'Help': the desk puts in the cents for interest, "there is nothing to fetch" (005-tap_Help.png). Went back.
- Tapped '$1', 'Groceries', 'In a week'. A line showed: $1.00 now, at most $0.01 interest, no fees, and the message it would send (009-tap_In_a_week.png).
- Tapped 'Ask for this loan'. A WhatsApp-style chat: "The credit officer is reading your message…" (010).
- Waited 15 s. Officer said yes, limit $1, cents put in my wallet (011-wait_15s.png).
- Tapped 'Borrow $1.00 now'. "Done. You now owe $1.00, due by Dec 17, 2026." Wallet $1.10 (012-tap_Borrow_1_00_now.png).
- Opened 'Receipt': date, time, amount, and codes "0x77e5…1b27", "0x6bae6d6f…" (013).
- 'OK', then 'More': Send money, Receive, Back a friend. Receive was hidden there (015-tap_More.png).
- 'Receive': a long code, and "You never need to fetch test dollars yourself" (016-tap_Receive.png).
- Next day (Nov 18): "You owe today $1.00, plus 0.03 of a cent" (018-days_1.png).
- 'Pay back', 'Pay back everything'. "Loan closed. You paid $1.00. Your wallet now has $0.10. You owe nothing" (020-tap_Pay_back_everything.png).

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1. After 'Get started' I expected a Receive screen for test dollars. There was none; the app went straight to the loan form. I spent three steps (scroll, Help, back) hunting for it before learning I did not need it. Slowed me.
- F2. "Receive" is folded under "More" and only exists once you have a wallet, so a friend's "go there first" cannot be followed. Annoyed me.
- F3. The date jumped from Nov 03 to Nov 17 between two screens with no explanation. Made me doubt the due date for a moment. Tiny.
- F4. Pay-back choices start at "In a week"; I wanted to say "tomorrow". Tiny.
- F5. The receipt codes mean nothing to me and look like something I must keep. Tiny.
- F6. "You paid $1.00: the $1.00 you borrowed plus less than a cent of interest" reads as two different amounts. Wallet went $1.10 to $0.10, so it was $1.00. Tiny.
- F7. The officer said "Tap Borrow now"; the button says "Borrow $1.00 now". Tiny.

## 4. Did I get the loan? Did I pay it back?
Yes: $1.00 in about a minute. Yes: paid all of it the next day with one tap. Screen says loan closed, I owe nothing, no due date, I can borrow $1.00 again. $0.10 of the desk's cents is left.

## 5. Anything that felt unsafe, like real money, or dishonest
Nothing felt like real money; the banner and "test dollars" are everywhere. "What you write is public on the test network" was a bit alarming, but it warned me not to write personal details. The AI officer saying yes in seconds felt too easy, like nobody checked anything. The long Receive code looked like a bank number to hide, but the page said to show it to friends.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. Say on the first screen that I never need to fetch test dollars, before I go hunting for Receive.
2. Put "Receive" and "Send money" in plain view, not under "More".
3. Let me pick "tomorrow" as a pay-back plan, and make the final "You paid" line one clear number.
