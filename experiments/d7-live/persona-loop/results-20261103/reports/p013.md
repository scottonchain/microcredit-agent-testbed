# Arjun, 27, home carer. Test report.

## 1. What I wanted
A small loan, about $5, for stock for my market stall. Pay it back on the last day. My friend sent the link.

## 2. What happened, step by step
- Opened the link. Yellow bar: "practice version, test tokens, not real money". Pressed Get started (001-look.png).
- A form: how much, what for, when to pay back (003-wait_5s.png). My finger slipped on $1 first (004-tap_1.png), then I chose $5. Green box: "$5.00 now... $0.04 at most... No other fees." Clear (005-tap_5.png).
- No choice for "stock to sell", so "Something else" and I wrote "stock for market stall". "In 30 days". Pressed Ask for this loan.
- "Done" box, with a Receipt full of codes (009-tap_Ask_for_this_loan.png, 010-tap_Receipt.png).
- Officer answered in a minute: "You can borrow up to $5... Tap Borrow" AND "no free stake left, try again later", same message (011-tap_OK.png). No Borrow button anywhere (012-scroll_down.png).
- I asked which one it is. It said sorry for the mixed message, then wrote the mixed message again (016-wait_15s.png).
- Wallet page: "Test dollars $0.00", "You can borrow up to $0.00". No sign of my request (017-tap_Back.png).
- Help explained wallet, backing, interest; not "stake" (020-tap_Help.png).
- Asked again. "Check back in a day or two." Came back 2 days later: $0.00 (029-tap_Back.png).
- Asked for less. "Yes from me, but no money today." It cut me to $2 itself. "If still $0 next week, the desk is failing you" (035-wait_15s.png).
- One week later: $0.00 (037-tap_Back.png). Send feedback on Help did nothing (040-tap_Send_feedback.png).
- Last message. "There is no other path I can offer" (047-wait_15s.png). I stopped.

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1. Credit desk. Expected a yes or a no. Saw yes and no in the same breath, three times, even after it said sorry for it. Told me to tap Borrow; no Borrow button exists. Blocked me.
- F2. Wallet page. Expected to see "you asked for $5, waiting". Saw $0.00 and $0.00 and nothing else. Only the chat knows my request. Had to go in and ask each time. Slowed me.
- F3. Words. "Stake", "new lines", "wallet 0x122E…6571", "recorded as 0xf9121d0d…", "what they lock". Help does not explain stake or lines. Annoyed me.
- F4. Officer said "I put a few cents in your wallet". Wallet shows $0.00. Said "it is set now". It was not. Annoyed me, and I stopped trusting it.
- F5. "Send feedback" on Help: tapped twice, nothing opened. Annoyed me.
- F6. A "Done" box with a receipt every time I send one chat line. Feels like a bank transfer, for a message. Tiny.
- F7. "Refresh the page": I do not know how on a phone. Back worked. Tiny.
- F8. The desk cut my amount to $2 without asking. I had offered it. Tiny.

## 4. Did I get the loan?
No. Nothing to pay back. I owe $0, nothing is due. The officer says a "$2 approval stays on record", but the wallet page shows nothing, so I do not believe it.

## 5. Unsafe, real money, dishonest?
- Not like real money: the yellow bar says practice everywhere, nobody asked for bank details or money. Good.
- Dishonest feeling: the officer said money was in my wallet and the loan was set, and the screen said $0.00. Saying sorry and then repeating the same thing is worse than the first mistake.
- "What you write is public": so I wrote nothing about myself. Fine, but cold.
- "Remove this wallet... save the code above first": I saw no code above. Did not tap.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. One answer only. If the desk has no money, say "No loan today. We will tell you when." Never say "tap Borrow" when there is no Borrow.
2. Put my request on the wallet page: "Asked $5 on this date. Approved $2. Waiting for desk money." I should not have to chat to find out.
3. Plain words. No "stake", "lines", or 0x codes on my screen. And make Send feedback open something.
