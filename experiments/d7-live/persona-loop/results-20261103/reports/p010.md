# Hana, 22, delivery rider: report

## 1. What I wanted
About $6 to top up my prepaid meter, from a link a friend sent, on my work laptop. Borrow it, pay it back on the last day.

## 2. What happened, step by step
- First screen: "borrow up to $5 of test dollars", Get started, fingerprint talk, a QR code. Nowhere to put my $6. (002-look.png)
- Get started: "One moment…" (003)
- Form: $1/$2/$3/$5, what for, when. Picked $5, A bill, In 30 days. Green box: get $5.00, pay $0.04 at most. Clear. (007-tap_In_30_days.png)
- Ask for this loan. "Your request is with the credit officer." (008)
- Officer answered in 10 seconds: yes, "Tap Borrow whenever you're ready." (010-wait_10s.png)
- No Borrow button. Scrolled: nothing. (011)
- Back. Wallet: $0.00, "You can borrow up to $0.00". (012-tap_Back.png)
- Waited 25 s. Still $0.00. More: Send money, Receive, Back a friend. (015)
- Help says the desk vouches, then you borrow. Back: still $0.00. (016-tap_Help.png)
- Wrote to the desk that it shows $0 and I can't find Borrow. (020)
- Officer: "Sorry, my approval didn't go through on your side, I'm setting it again." A "Borrow $5.00 now" button appeared under the chat. (021-wait_15s.png)
- Tapped it. "You now owe $5.00, due by Jun 6, 2027." Wallet $5.10. (023-tap_Borrow_5_00_now.png)
- Came back on Jun 6. "You owe today $5.04. Due today." (025-days_30.png)
- Pay back, Pay back everything. "Loan closed. You paid $5.04. Wallet $0.06. Limit now $5.02." (027-tap_Pay_back_everything.png)
- Receipt: "Nov 3, 2026, 2:04 PM: Pay back, from wallet 0x74EF…906A." (028-tap_Receipt.png)

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1. Officer said yes and "Tap Borrow", but there was no Borrow anywhere and the wallet said $0.00 through a minute of waiting, More and Help. I had to complain before it worked, and it admitted the approval "didn't go through". Blocked me. In a hurry at work I would have closed it.
- F2. First screen: I expected an amount box; I got Get started, fingerprint talk and a QR code. Also "up to $5" when I need $6, and "nothing to buy with it", so it cannot top up my meter anyway. Annoyed me.
- F3. Receipt says Nov 3, 2026, but the app said today was Jun 6, 2027, the due date. The phone's date also jumped (Nov 3, Mar 29, Apr 27, May 7) between taps. Annoyed me.
- F4. On the laptop, "You owe today" and Pay back sit below the bottom edge; balance, limit and "Who vouches for you" come first. Had to scroll to find how to pay. Slowed me.
- F5. Words I did not get: "wallet 0x74EF…906A", "Recorded on the test network as 0xe0f42d96…", "the approval is a cent or two higher", "interest counts toward your own credit". Tiny.
- F6. The chat showed "I need $5 for a bill…" as if I typed it; the form wrote it. And "what you write is public on the test network" made me nervous to write anything. Tiny.

## 4. Did I get the loan? Did I pay it back?
Yes: $5.00, due Jun 6, 2027. Paid $5.04 on Jun 6, on time. The app says I owe nothing, wallet $0.06, limit $5.02, nothing due.

## 5. Anything unsafe, like real money, or dishonest
Every screen said practice money, so it never felt like real money. What felt dishonest: the officer saying "you're set" when nothing was set, then "sorry, it didn't go through on your side"; after that I did not trust what it said next. The receipt date not matching the app's own "today" felt wrong.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. Don't let the officer say "Tap Borrow" until the button exists. Put Borrow right in the chat and check the approval really went through.
2. Let me type my own amount, or put "max $5" and the amount buttons on the very first screen, not after Get started.
3. Put "You owe / Pay back" at the top of the wallet, make the receipt date match today, and hide the 0x codes behind "details".
