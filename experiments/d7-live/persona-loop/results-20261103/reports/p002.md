# Fatima, 22, call centre: $3 for the meter

## 1. What I wanted
About $3 to top up my prepaid electricity meter. A friend sent the link. I wanted to borrow it, pay some now and the rest after payday. Never used crypto; burned by hidden fees once.

## 2. What happened, step by step
- Opened the link. Yellow banner: "test tokens, not real money, nothing here is a real loan" (001-look.png). Puzzled: my friend said loan app.
- Get started: nothing for a few seconds, then a "What do you need?" form (003-wait_5s.png).
- Picked $3, "A bill", "In two weeks" (006-tap_In_two_weeks.png). Looked under the button for fees: nothing.
- "Ask for this loan": fingerprint box, "went through" (008, 009).
- Credit desk: "reading your message…" (013-wait_20s.png). Waited about 1 min 40 s. Nothing.
- Sent "Hello? Is anyone there?". "Went through", but my message never showed in the chat (018-tap_OK.png).
- Tapped Back. "Your wallet": $0.10, "borrow up to $3.00", "Backed by 0x8E2B…e41b $3.00", "Interest 9.33% a year" (020-tap_Back.png).
- Borrow. Typed "3.o0" by mistake. Button grey, no message (022-type_How_much_.png). Saw the o, retyped 3.
- "$3.00 now, owe about $3.03 in 30 days." Borrow: "Due on 1/31/2027" (024-tap_Borrow.png).
- Wallet $3.10, owe $3.00 (026-tap_Back.png). Pay only part, 1: "Paid $1.00. Still owed $2.00" (030-tap_Pay_this_part.png).
- 10 days later: owe $2.01, due in 20 days (033-days_10.png). Pay back everything: "Loan closed. You paid $2.01: $3.00 borrowed plus about $-0.99 interest. $0.00 of that interest counts toward your own credit" (034-tap_Pay_back_everything.png).
- Receipt is only a link "see the record" (036). Wallet $0.09, no loan (038-tap_Back.png).
- Opened Credit desk at the end: the officer HAD answered both messages and said it backed me and put cents in my wallet for interest (040-tap_Credit_desk.png). I never saw that while waiting.

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
F1. Credit desk. Expected a reply "within a minute". Saw "reading…" for nearly 3 minutes; the replies only showed when I came back at the end. Nearly gave up. Almost blocked me.
F2. Same screen. My "Hello?" said "went through" but never appeared. Thought it was lost. Annoyed me.
F3. Wallet. "Backed by 0x8E2B…e41b $3.00": who is that, do I owe them? No explanation. Confused me.
F4. Front page "no fees"; wallet "9.33% a year". Two stories. It cost 1 cent, but say so up front. Annoyed me.
F5. Borrow. I asked for two weeks; it says "30 days" and "due 1/31/2027". It is early November, so 30 days is not end of January. Which is real? Confused me.
F6. Borrow box. "3.o0" just greyed the button, no hint. Tiny.
F7. Pay-off box. "$-0.99 interest", "$0.00 counts toward your own credit". Minus interest? I paid $1 + $2.01 = $3.01. Reads like a wrong bill. Annoyed me, felt wrong.
F8. "Receipt" is a link, not a breakdown. Tiny.
F9. Words I did not get: "test network", "backing", "Receive", "recovery code". Tiny.

## 4. Did I get the loan?
Yes, $3.00 arrived straight away. Paid $1.00 the same day and $2.01 ten days later. App says loan closed, I owe nothing. Total cost 1 cent ($0.10 before, $0.09 after). Had I not paid: due 1/31/2027.

## 5. Unsafe, real money, or dishonest
- "$-0.99 interest" looks like a wrong bill; on a real loan I would screenshot and argue.
- "No fees" next to "9.33% a year".
- "Backed" by an unknown code with no explanation of what that means for me.
- "Public on the test network": I wrote "electricity meter" before thinking.
- Nothing felt like real money leaving me; the banner is everywhere. But then I doubt this app could ever get real $3 to my meter.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. Make the chat show the officer's reply when it arrives (or buzz me), and show my own messages after sending.
2. Put the cost in cents on the first screen ("$3 for 30 days costs about 3 cents"); drop "no fees" if there is interest.
3. Fix the pay-off message so it adds up ("You paid $3.01 in total: $3.00 borrowed + $0.01 interest") and explain "backed by" in plain words.
