# Amara, 27, mechanic's helper: report

## 1. What I wanted
A small loan, about $5, for a late electricity bill. My friend sent the link and said it was a loan app.

## 2. What happened, step by step
- Opened the link. "Try a small loan", "Borrow up to $5 of test dollars", yellow strip "test tokens, not real money". I did not know those words (001-look.png).
- Tapped "Get started". "One moment…" with nothing to tap, then a form: how much, what for, when to pay back (003-wait_5s.png).
- Picked $5, A bill, In two weeks. It said about $0.02 interest.
- Tapped "Write to the credit desk in your own words" and asked what test dollars means.
- Waited 15 seconds. The desk (an AI) said: practice money, no real value, cannot pay my bill (010-wait_15s.png).
- Said I would try anyway. The desk vouched for me, put a few cents in my wallet, and a "Borrow $5.00 now" button appeared.
- Tapped it. Instantly "Done. You now owe $5.00, due by Dec 3, 2026" (014-tap_Borrow_5_00_now.png). No fingerprint prompt that I noticed.
- Wallet: $5.10, limit $5.00, "backed by The credit desk", owe $5.00 (016-scroll_down.png).
- Two weeks later: owed $5.02, due in 16 days.
- Pay back, Pay back everything: "Loan closed. You paid $5.02". Wallet $0.08, limit $5.01 (020-tap_Pay_back_everything.png).
- Tapped "Tell us how it went", sent a note, "Thank you". Done.

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1. First screen. Expected a loan app; saw "test dollars", "test tokens", "practice money". I had to ask the desk to learn it cannot pay my bill, four screens in. Blocked me (from what I came for).
- F2. "One moment…" after Get started, nothing to tap for a few seconds; I thought it was stuck. Tiny.
- F3. Wallet page. Wanted Help for "test dollars" and "backed by the credit desk". Help was top right on earlier screens; the wallet page top had none, so I gave up. (There is a Help button at the very bottom, but I had stopped looking.) Slowed me.
- F4. Wallet showed $5.10 after borrowing $5.00. Small text said the desk added cents for interest; I was unsure if the 10 cents was mine. Annoyed me.
- F5. After paying, limit went from $5.00 to $5.01, no explanation. Tiny.
- F6. "Who vouches for you: The credit desk". I do not know who that is or why an AI would cover my loan. Annoyed me.
- F7. "Anyone on the test network can read this". What is a test network, who is on it? Annoyed me.

## 4. Did I get the loan?
Practice money only, so no, not the loan I needed. I paid the practice loan back after two weeks: $5.02. The screen says I owe nothing, nothing is due, and I can borrow up to $5.01 again.

## 5. Anything that felt unsafe, like real money, or dishonest
- The app says everywhere it is not real money, which is honest. But "loan", "borrow", "interest", "due by Dec 3" all feel real, and my friend thought it was.
- The loan was made instantly, no fingerprint or "are you sure" step I saw. For real money I would want one.
- It says what I write is public. I do not know who reads it, so I was careful.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. First screen, big plain words: "This is pretend money. You cannot pay a bill with it." Not "test tokens".
2. Help in the same place on every screen, at the top, with one line each for "test dollars", "test network" and "backed by the credit desk".
3. Tell me where the real loan is, or when it will exist, so I know whether to come back.
