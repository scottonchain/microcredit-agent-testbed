# Chloe, 19 — report

## 1. What I wanted
A friend sent the link. I needed about $5 for a deposit on a rented sewing machine, paying part now and the rest later. I've never used crypto and don't know what a wallet is.

## 2. What happened, step by step
- Opened link: "Try a small loan", yellow banner says practice money, not real. Letdown, I needed real money. (001-look.png)
- Get started → spinner → a form (how much, what for, when). Said "wallet opens with your fingerprint" but never asked for my finger. (003)
- Looked for a "Pay back" button first. None. Help says you can pay in parts, not where. (005)
- "Back" from Help landed on "Your wallet": "You can borrow up to $0.00". Scary. "More" had Send money, Receive, Back a friend; no Pay back. (006, 007)
- Ask for a loan, tapped $5: Help opened instead, twice. Third try worked. (009, 012, 015)
- Work tools, In two weeks. Clear summary: $5 now, ~$0.02 interest, $0.04 max, no fees. (017)
- Ask for this loan → chat with "credit officer (an AI)". After 15 s it agreed and said it put a few cents in my wallet for interest. (019)
- Borrow $5.00 now → "Done. You owe $5.00, due Dec 3, 2026." Wallet $5.10. No fingerprint prompt. (020)
- Pay back now exists. Pay only part, typed 2 → "Paid $2.00. Still owed: $3.00." (025)
- Came back 10 days later: opened straight to my wallet, owe $3.01, due in 20 days. (027)
- Pay back everything → "Loan closed… $5.00 plus 1.28 cents interest. Wallet $0.09. You owe nothing." (029)
- Receipt: "wallet 0x7406…9976, recorded as 0xdc918dde…". No idea. (030)

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1. Form: tapping $5 opened Help, twice. Expected the $5 button to light up. Maybe my cracked screen, but only that button did it. Slowed me.
- F2. Help "‹ Back" goes to "Your wallet", not the form I came from, so I restarted the form each time. Slowed me.
- F3. "Your wallet" before any loan: "You can borrow up to $0.00". I thought I'd been refused before asking. Annoyed me, nearly quit.
- F4. No "Pay back" button before a loan and nothing says it appears later. Only after paying off did it say "When you owe something, a Pay back button appears here". Annoyed me.
- F5. "Opens with your fingerprint" but it never asked, even when borrowing. Good or anyone can do it? Tiny but unsettling.
- F6. Receipt codes "0x7406…9976", "public record site", "not a robot": meaningless. Tiny.
- F7. "Test network", "backing", "stake", "vouches", "desk locks up test dollars": Help explains, I still don't really get it. Tiny.

## 4. Did I get the loan? Did I pay it back?
Yes: $5 of practice money at once. Paid $2 same day, $3.01 ten days later. Screen says loan closed, I owe nothing, $0.09 left, can borrow up to $5.01 again. Nothing due.

## 5. Anything unsafe, like real money, or dishonest
The banner on every screen made it clear it isn't real: honest but disappointing. The $5.10 "wallet" and "Send money" button felt like a real bank. Saying "what you write is public, no names" was good. Never being asked for a fingerprint felt odd for a "wallet".

## 6. Three things I would change
1. Don't show "borrow up to $0.00" before I've asked; say "Ask the credit desk to find out your limit".
2. Make Help's Back return to where I was, and move the Help link away from the buttons (or make the $ buttons bigger).
3. Say on the first page and in Help that a Pay back button appears once you owe something, and hide the 0x codes under "technical details".
