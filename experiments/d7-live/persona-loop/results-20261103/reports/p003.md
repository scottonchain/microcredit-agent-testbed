# Victor, 38, shop assistant — test report

## 1. What I wanted
A small loan, about $8, to pay a late electricity bill before they cut me off, paid back on the last day when my wages come in. I asked for $20.

## 2. What happened, step by step
- Opened my friend's link: blank white screen. Waited, back, reload. Still white (001-look.png, 009-look.png).
- Tried the phone's "home" button: the app finally appeared, "Omnequa test wallet" (016-look.png).
- Tapped the big green "Create my wallet". Fingerprint, done. Wallet: $0.00, "borrow up to $0.00" (018-wait_5s.png).
- Tapped Borrow, typed 8. Button stayed grey; orange box said ask the credit desk (020-type_How_much_.png).
- Wrote to the desk: need $20 for the bill, pay back end of month. A "went through" box with a "block explorer" link (024).
- Desk answered in seconds: $5 is the test limit, due in 30 days, a few cents interest (026-wait_15s.png).
- Borrow, Max, Borrow. "Borrow $5.00 went through. Due on 12/14/2026" (029-tap_Borrow.png).
- Wallet showed $5.10, not $5.00. Why? (031-tap_Back.png)
- Tapped Help. Long page: recovery code, add to home screen, "Remove this wallet from this phone" (033-tap_Help.png).
- Thumb landed on Remove. Box: "Remove wallet from this phone?" with a big green Remove (035). Panicked, tapped "Keep it".
- Put the phone away 30 days. Came back: "Overdue by 1 day. Pay now." and backers could lose their stake (038-days_30.png).
- Pay back, "Pay back everything". "Loan closed. You paid $5.04" (040-tap_Pay_back_everything.png).
- Wallet: $0.06 left, can borrow $5.02.

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1. First screen: expected the app, got a blank white page for over half a minute; reload did nothing, only "home" worked. Blocked me.
- F2. Home said "Due in 30 days (12/14/2026)". I came back 30 days later: overdue by 1 day, and people who backed me could lose their stake. I did what it said. Annoyed me; felt unfair and scary.
- F3. Help page: "Remove this wallet from this phone" sits among ordinary buttons, and in the warning the dangerous "Remove" is the big green button while "Keep it" is small and plain. It asked first, so nothing was lost. Scared me badly.
- F4. Borrowed $5.00, wallet showed $5.10. Nothing explains the extra 10 cents. Tiny, but unexplained money makes me nervous.
- F5. Asked for $20, got $5. The desk explained well, and the first message did say "up to $5"; I just did not read it. Slowed me.
- F6. Words I did not understand: passkey, USDC, faucet, block explorer, "0x0446…e47d", "Backed by 0x8E2B…e41b". Annoyed me.
- F7. Borrow box said interest about $0.04 but also "keep a few dollars for the interest". Which? Tiny.
- F8. "Send feedback: tell us what confused you" has no box and no button. Tiny.

## 4. Did I get the loan? Did I pay it back?
Yes, $5 (not the $8 I needed). I paid $5.04 on day 30, which the app counted as one day late. It now says I owe nothing, I have $0.06, and can borrow $5.02 again. No due date.

## 5. Unsafe, real-money or dishonest feelings
- The banner says nothing here is a real loan. Then why send me this for a real bill? Never found out.
- The Remove-wallet button, with Remove highlighted, felt one slip from losing my money.
- "Overdue" on the day it said it was due, plus backers losing their stake, felt like a trap.
- The desk says everything I write is public; I had already written about my job and bill.
- The recovery code is printed in full on the Help page for anyone looking over my shoulder.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. Say the last day I can pay without being late, plainly ("Pay by 12/13"), and if you say "30 days", make day 30 on time.
2. Move "Remove this wallet" away from Help and make "Keep it" the big button.
3. Fix the blank first page, use normal words (no faucet, passkey, block explorer, 0x codes), and explain any money in my wallet I did not borrow.
