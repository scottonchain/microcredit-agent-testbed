# Kofi, 27, shop assistant — report

## 1. What I wanted
A friend sent me a link. I needed about $8 for a school uniform and wanted to pay it back the next day. I have never used crypto or a "wallet".

## 2. What happened, step by step
1. Opened the link. Yellow banner: "Practice version... not real money." (002-look.png)
2. Tapped "Get started". Nothing changed; after a wait it jumped to "What do you need?". (004-wait_5s.png)
3. Biggest amount was $5 "in this test"; I needed $8. Picked $5, School, "In a week". Box: get $5.00, pay back about $5.04 in 30 days. Clear. (007-tap_In_a_week.png)
4. Tapped "Ask for this loan". "Done" box; the "Receipt" only said "see the record". (009-tap_Receipt.png)
5. Chat answered in seconds: "You are set for $5... A few cents for the interest are already in your wallet." What wallet? (011-wait_15s.png)
6. "Borrow $5.00 now", then "Borrow". "You now owe about $5.00. Due by Mar 12, 2027." (013-tap_Borrow.png)
7. Page "Your wallet": $5.10 in it, I owe $5.00. Now I understood "wallet". (014-tap_OK.png)
8. Looked for a way to pay the shop. Help: "play money". More > "Send money" wants an address "starting with 0x". Gave up. (019-tap_Send_money.png)
9. Wallet already said "29 days". Next day: "28 days", I owe $5.01. (021-days_1.png)
10. "Pay back everything". At once: "Loan closed. You paid $5.01." Wallet $0.10. (023-tap_Pay_back_everything.png)
11. "see the record": another website, "Performing security verification", red "Incompatible browser extension", "Ray ID". Never loaded. (026-wait_10s.png)
12. Wallet: no loan, "You can borrow up to $5.00". (028-tap_OK.png)

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1. First screen: I wanted a loan for a uniform; I got "practice", "test dollars". Honest, but nothing says where the real thing is. Blocked my real purpose.
- F2. Only $5; I needed $8. No way to ask for more. Slowed me.
- F3. "Get started" seemed to do nothing, then changed by itself. Annoyed me.
- F4. "Wallet" is used in the tab title and by the officer before I had seen one. Annoyed me.
- F5. No way to get the money to a shop; "Send money" wants a "0x" address. Blocked, if this were real.
- F6. "Backing — Backed by The credit desk $5.00": no idea what that is. Tiny.
- F7. "30 days" right after borrowing, "29 days" a minute later, "28 days" next morning. Annoyed me.
- F8. "You paid $5.01: $5.00 plus less than a cent of interest" does not add up; $5.10 minus $5.01 is $0.09 but the wallet shows $0.10; "$0.00 of that interest counts toward your own credit" means nothing to me. Annoyed me.
- F9. The "receipt" is a link to another website that showed an error wall. No receipt I can read. Slowed me, looked alarming.
- F10. "Public on the test network" and "A small buffer is approved": who sees it, who approved what? Tiny.

## 4. Did I get the loan? Did I pay it back?
Yes: $5.00 of test dollars (not the $8, not real). I paid $5.01 the next day. The app says the loan is closed, I owe nothing, $0.10 is left, and I can borrow $5 again. Before I paid it was due by Mar 12, 2027.

## 5. Anything unsafe, like real money, or dishonest
Nothing dishonest: every screen said "not real money", and "$5.00 now, $5.04 at most" was clear. What felt off: the fingerprint happened without me seeing anything; "public on the test network"; something "approved" for more than I owe; cents not adding up; the record link landing on a strange site with a red warning.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. First screen: "You cannot buy anything with this, it is practice only," and where a real loan is, if any. Let me type $8, or say why only $5.
2. Explain "wallet", "backing" and "buffer" the first time they appear, one plain line each.
3. A real receipt in the app (date, amount paid, what I owe now) with cents that add up, not a link to another website that does not open.
