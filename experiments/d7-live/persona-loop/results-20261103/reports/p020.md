# Isla, 34, home carer: report (p020)

## 1. What I wanted
About $3, real money, for a tool I need for a job on Friday. A friend sent the link; I used my work laptop. Burned by hidden fees before, I wanted every cost up front.

## 2. What happened, step by step
- Opened the link: "Try a small loan", up to $5 of "test dollars", "nothing here is a real loan". Looked right on the big screen: card centred, square code at right (001-look.png).
- Tapped Get started. "One moment…", nothing to tap, then the form (003-wait_5s.png).
- Picked $3, Work tools, In two weeks. Green box: $3.00 now, about $0.02 interest, $0.03 at most, no other fees (006-tap_In_two_weeks.png). Clear.
- Tapped Ask for this loan: "The credit officer is reading your message…" (007).
- Called to a client; closed the app, came back next day. Answer waiting: approved, "pay back about $3.00 by Dec 3, 2026" (008-days_1.png).
- Tapped Borrow $3.00 now. Done box: owe $3.00, "due by Dec 7, 2027" (009). Receipt full of codes like "0x4656…6A15" (010).
- Wallet: $3.10 in it, owe $3.00, due in 30 days (011).
- More > Send money: only to "your friend's address … starts with 0x" (014).
- Help: "Test dollars have no value", plus a warning about shared computers (016).
- Back to the wallet: suddenly "Late by 5 days", owe $3.03 (018-back.png).
- Pay back everything: "Loan closed. You paid $3.03", wallet $0.07, limit $3.01 (020).
- Help > found the recovery code (023) > Remove this wallet > Remove it. Back at the start (026).

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
F1. Start page and Help: I expected money I could spend on a tool. It is practice money "worth nothing". Honest, but useless for Friday. Blocked my real goal.
F2. Offer said "by Dec 3, 2026"; confirmation said "due by Dec 7, 2027". Different day and year, unexplained. Annoyed me; I stopped trusting the dates.
F3. Wallet after Help: one tap earlier "due in 30 days", the next "Late by 5 days" and $3.03 owed. I did nothing in between. The same box still said "about $3.03 by the due date if you wait until then" while calling me late. Felt like being burned again. Scared me; I paid at once.
F4. Receipt: "wallet 0x4656…6A15", "0x220b4eb7…". No idea what these are. Tiny.
F5. Send money wants a friend's "0x" address. No shop, no bank. Blocked me, even had the money been real.
F6. "Save the code above first", but the code was hidden in a closed section. Slowed me.
F7. Recovery code is a huge block of letters; "keep it private", but on a work laptop I have nowhere to keep it. Annoyed me.
F8. "0.8% a month" everywhere, then "9.33% a year" only after I paid. Tiny.

## 4. Did I get the loan? Did I pay it back?
Yes: $3.00 of test dollars, at once. Paid back $3.03 (2.68 cents interest). The screen says I owe nothing; limit now $3.01. No late fee despite the "5 days late". No real $3, so the tool money must come from elsewhere.

## 5. Unsafe, real-money or dishonest feelings
- "Late by 5 days" with no action from me felt dishonest, even for 3 cents.
- Due dates that do not match between screens.
- "Anyone who can unlock this computer could open it": I am on a shared work laptop and only learned this from Help, after borrowing.
- "What you write is public": fine for a test; on a real loan I would not want my reasons public.
- Nothing felt like real money: the banner says so on every page. Good.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. Say on the first screen, in big letters, that this money cannot buy anything or go to a shop or bank.
2. One due date, the same on every screen; never show "late" or more owed without saying what happened and when.
3. Warn about shared computers before the wallet is made, and put "remove this wallet" on the wallet screen, not under Help > Done testing.
