# Lucia, 24, apprentice electrician. Phone test.

## 1. What I wanted
About $5 for the deposit on a sewing machine I rent; get it, pay it back the next day. A friend sent the link.

## 2. What happened, step by step
- First screen "A small test loan", yellow banner "not real money", no amount anywhere (002-look.png).
- Tapped "Get started": same screen came back (003). Waited 5 s, then "What do you need?" with $1 $2 $3 $5 (004-wait_5s.png). This is what I wanted first.
- Tapped $5, "Something else", wrote "deposit for a sewing machine I rent", "In a week" (007-tap_In_a_week.png). Green box: get $5.00, pay back about $5.04 within 30 days.
- "Ask for this loan": box "your request is with the credit officer" (009). No fingerprint was asked, though the first screen promised one.
- Waited 15 s. The officer (an AI) approved $5 (011-wait_15s.png): "a few cents for the interest are already in your wallet".
- "Borrow $5.00 now": a screen asked "How much?" again with 25% / 50% / Max (012). Tapped "Borrow".
- "Borrow $5.00: done. You owe about $5.00, due by Mar 12, 2027" (013-tap_Borrow.png). Wallet $5.10.
- "Pay back" (016-tap_Pay_back.png): "You owe right now $5.00". Tapped "Pay back everything".
- "Loan closed. You paid $5.00 plus less than a cent of interest" (017). Wallet $0.10. Receipt is only a link "see the record"; I did not open it.
- Next day (021-days_1.png): wallet $0.10, "You can borrow up to $5.00", nothing owed.
- Looked at Help (023-tap_Help.png) and More (025-tap_More.png). Stopped.

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1. First screen. Expected: how much I can borrow and a borrow button. Saw: "test loan", "wallet", "recovery code", "home-screen app", not real money. I thought it was a game. Slowed me.
- F2. "Get started" showed the same page again for a second (003). Tiny.
- F3. I chose "In a week" but the green box still says "within 30 days". Which date is mine? Tiny.
- F4. "A few cents for the interest are already in your wallet", and after borrowing $5 the wallet had $5.10. I never asked for 10 cents; nobody said who put it there or why. Slowed me.
- F5. Borrow screen asked "How much?" again with 25% / 50% / Max after I and the officer both said $5. What is 25% of a loan? Annoyed me.
- F6. Pay back screen: "A small buffer is approved so the interest that builds up while this is sent is covered." I do not know "buffer"; it sounded like they can take more than I owe. Then "paid $5.00 plus less than a cent", but the wallet went $5.10 to $0.10: exactly $5? Did I pay interest or not? Annoyed me.
- F7. "Backing", "Backed by The credit desk $5.00", Help says "a small line". I do not know what backing or a line is. Tiny.

## 4. Did I get the loan?
Yes, $5.00. Paid back the same day, $5.00 plus less than a cent; "Loan closed". Next day: nothing owed, I can borrow $5 again. I owe nothing. The due date would have been Mar 12, 2027.

## 5. Unsafe, like real money, or dishonest
- Nothing felt like real money; the banner says so everywhere. But I needed a real $5 and the app never says where that is.
- "What you write here is public on the test network." I wrote about my sewing machine before I read that. A bit scary that strangers can read it.
- Interest was honest and small ($0.04 max, under a cent for a day). Nothing felt dishonest.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. First screen: "You can borrow up to $5" and one big "Borrow" button; the test warning in one line, not paragraphs about wallets and recovery codes.
2. Do not ask "how much" twice. After the officer says yes, one button "Get $5". Remove 25% / 50% / Max.
3. Say the 10 cents plainly: "We put 10 cents in your wallet to pay the interest, so you only pay back $5." Remove the word "buffer".
