# Ivy, 68, warehouse picker. Needed $2 for a bus pass.

## 1. What I wanted
Two dollars for a bus pass until payday in two weeks. And before pressing anything, the total I'd pay back. I got stung by hidden fees once.

## 2. What happened
1. Opened the link. Yellow line: "Practice version, not real money. Nothing here is a real loan." My friend said it was a loan app. (001-look.png)
2. Tapped Get started. Nothing for a few seconds, no spinner (002), then a form. (003-wait_5s.png)
3. Picked $2, Transport, In two weeks. No total, no due date, nothing below. (006, 007)
4. Would not press "Ask for this loan" blind. Tapped "Write to the credit desk in your own words instead." (008)
5. Asked what $2 costs in total. Got a "Done" box with a "Receipt" for a chat message. (010)
6. Waited 15 seconds. Answer: about 1.5 cents interest, pay back about $2.02, no other fees. Then: "The officer backs you with $2.00 and put a few cents in your wallet." (012, 013)
7. Tapped the pale "Borrow $2.00 now." Borrow page: "You receive $2.00 now. In 30 days you owe about $2.02." Clear. Tapped Borrow. (014)
8. "Went through. You now owe about $2.00. Due on 1/13/2027." Receipt only said "see the record." (015, 016)
9. OK: same page now "$0.00 available", orange "You have no credit yet." (017)
10. "Your wallet": $2.10 in wallet, owe $2.01, "Due in 27 days, by 1/13/2027", "Backed by 0x8E2B…e41b", "Network fees are paid for you during the test." (018)
11. More, then Help: basics only. (021)
12. Pay back: owe $2.01, "a small buffer is approved." Pay back everything: "Loan closed. You paid $2.00, about $0.00 interest." (023, 024)
13. Wallet: $0.10 left, can borrow $2.00 again. (026)

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1. The ask form: expected the total and due date by the Ask button; saw nothing. Had to go round through the chat. Slowed me.
- F2. Dates: "30 days" on Borrow, "due 1/13/2027" in the Done box, "due in 27 days" a minute later. It is early November; January 13 is over two months away. Annoyed me and dented my trust.
- F3. The amount wobbled: $2.02, then $2.00, then $2.01, then "paid $2.00, about $0.00 interest." I count cents now. Annoyed me.
- F4. Right after "went through", the Borrow page said "$0.00 available, you have no credit yet" in orange. I thought it had failed. Slowed me, scared me.
- F5. "Network fees are paid for you during the test" after the first page said no fees. What are they, and after the test? Annoyed me a lot.
- F6. Words I did not get: "wallet", "backs you", "0x8E2B…e41b", "$0.00 of that interest counts toward your own credit", "see the record", a "Receipt" for a message. Tiny, but they pile up.
- F7. I asked for two weeks; it became 30 days. "Borrow $2.00 now" is a pale button beside a big green Send. Tiny.

## 4. Did I get the loan?
Yes, $2.00 arrived. I paid it back the same sitting. It says the loan is closed and I owe nothing. Before paying it said due in 27 days, by 1/13/2027. I have 10 cents left the officer gave me, which I don't understand. Practice money, so no bus pass.

## 5. Unsafe, like real money, or dishonest
Nothing felt like a scam; the banner on every screen was honest. What bothered me: "network fees" after "no fees"; "a small buffer is approved" without a figure; "what you write is public"; numbers and dates that disagree. That is how the last app started.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. Put the total I pay back and the due date on the first form, by the Ask button.
2. One number and one date, the same on every screen, with the date really 30 days from today.
3. Say what "network fees" are and who pays after the test. And after I borrow, say "you've used your $2, pay it back to borrow again", not "you have no credit yet."
