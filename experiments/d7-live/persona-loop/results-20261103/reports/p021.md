# Idris, 34, tailor. Report on the test wallet app.

## 1. What I wanted
About $2 to top up my prepaid meter, paid back the next day. A friend sent the link and said "go to Receive first and get test dollars". My eyes are bad; I use large text.

## 2. What happened, step by step
- Opened the link. Big clear page, "Try a small loan", yellow strip: practice money only (001-look.png).
- Tapped "Get started". "One moment..." then straight into a loan form, "What do you need?" (003-wait_5s.png). No Receive anywhere.
- Tapped Help to find Receive. Long page of words, no Receive (004-tap_Help.png).
- Tapped Back. Only now saw "Your wallet": $0.00 (006-tap_Back.png).
- Opened "More", tapped Receive (008-tap_Receive.png): a black-and-white square, a long code, and "You never need to fetch test dollars yourself".
- Peeked at "Advanced: faucet": USDC, Circle, Base Sepolia. Back.
- Tapped "Ask for a loan", picked $2, "A bill", "In a week". Green box: you get $2.00, about $0.01 interest, no other fees (014-tap_In_a_week.png).
- Tapped "Ask for this loan". A chat with "the credit officer (an AI)". After 15 seconds it said yes (016-wait_15s.png).
- Tapped "Borrow $2.00 now". Done at once: owe $2.00, due Feb 20, 2028; wallet $2.10 (017-tap_Borrow_2_00_now.png).
- Closed the app with that Done box still open, never tapped OK.
- Next day, opened the app: straight to my wallet, owe $2.01, due in 29 days (019-days_1.png). Nothing lost.
- Tapped "Pay back", then "Pay back everything" (020-tap_Pay_back.png). "Loan closed... You owe nothing." Wallet $0.10 (021-tap_Pay_back_everything.png).
- Receipt: date, time, long codes (022-tap_Receipt.png). Tapped OK (023-tap_OK.png).

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1. "Get started" skipped the wallet and went straight to the loan form. I was hunting for Receive; it took Help, Back, then "More" to find it. Slowed me.
- F2. Help page: a wall of small grey words. "Vouches", "Backing", "Stake", "credit officer, an AI". Understood maybe half. Annoyed me.
- F3. Receive screen: the long code "0x4181..." is called an "address" but is not a street. Advanced says USDC, Circle, Base Sepolia; I know none of them. The line "you never need to fetch test dollars" helped, and says the opposite of my friend. Annoyed me.
- F4. The phone's date jumped from Nov 2026 to Jan 2028 right after "Get started". Tiny, but odd.
- F5. The money numbers disagree. Wallet: "You owe today $2.01". Pay-back page: "$2.01... plus 0.05 cents of interest" (less than a cent, so where is the extra cent from?). After paying: "You paid $2.00". Wallet went $2.10 to $0.10. A tailor counts cents. Annoyed me.
- F6. "The approval is a cent or two higher" on the pay-back page. What approval? Tiny.
- F7. The receipt is codes, plus a "public record site" that "may check that you are not a robot". I would never tap that. Tiny.

## 4. Did I get the loan? Did I pay it back?
Yes: $2.00 the moment I tapped Borrow. Yes: paid everything the next day. The screen says "You owe nothing" and shows no Pay back button. Due date was Feb 20, 2028; $0.10 of test dollars left.

## 5. Anything unsafe, like real money, or dishonest
- The yellow strip on every screen says practice money; it never felt like real money.
- The loan was made the instant I tapped, "your fingerprint confirms it", no pause to say yes again. With real money that is too fast.
- "Anyone on the test network can read this" made me careful. Honest, but unsettling.
- An AI decided in 15 seconds; I do not know from what. Nothing felt dishonest.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. After "Get started", show my wallet first, with Receive and Help on it, then let me ask for a loan from there.
2. Make the amounts agree: if I owe $2.01, say I paid $2.01, or say "$2.00 and less than a cent" everywhere.
3. Put the codes (0x..., USDC, Base Sepolia, faucet, public record) further away or explain each in one plain line; shorten Help and make its text bigger.
