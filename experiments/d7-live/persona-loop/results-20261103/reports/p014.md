# June, 49, mechanic's helper (tester 14)

## 1. What I wanted
A $5 loan for a bus pass until payday, paid back on the day it was due. I have never used crypto and feared the app would take real money.

## 2. What happened, step by step
- Opened the link. Yellow banner: "Practice version... not real money." Big text, easy on my eyes (001-look.png). But it says "nothing to buy with it"; I came for a real bus pass.
- Get started: no sign-up, no password. A form: how much, what for, when (004-wait_5s.png).
- Picked $5, Transport, In two weeks. Green box: about 2 cents interest, 4 at most, no other fees (007-tap_In_two_weeks.png). Reassuring.
- "Ask for this loan" gave a "Done" box: request is with the credit officer (008-tap_Ask_for_this_loan.png).
- Officer, in seconds: "Good news: you can borrow up to $5... Tap Borrow now. The desk has no free stake left right now; please try again later." Orange box: "no free stake... cannot grant new lines" (010-scroll_down.png). No Borrow button anywhere.
- Back: wallet $0.00, "You can borrow up to $0.00" (012-tap_Back.png). Help explains "Backing", not "stake" (013-tap_Help.png). More: Send money, Receive, Back a friend. No Borrow.
- Wrote to the desk. Reply: "I've set yours now... refresh your wallet... no free stake left; try again later" (020-wait_15s.png). Still $0.
- Next day: $0 (024-days_1.png). Wrote again. Reply: "display lag, not a no... try again later" (029-wait_15s.png). "Ask a friend to back you" shows a long code it calls my address (026-tap_Or_ask_a_friend_to_back_yo.png).
- Three days later: $0 (033-days_3.png). Fourth message. Reply: "set your borrow for real... that's on us... try again later" (037-wait_15s.png).
- Help: "Done testing?" only removes the wallet; "Send feedback" did nothing (042-tap_Send_feedback.png). Put the phone down.

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1. Credit desk. Expected a yes or a no. Got both in one message, four times over four days: "borrow $5, tap Borrow now" and "no free stake, try again later". No Borrow button exists; the wallet never left $0. Blocked me.
- F2. Orange box: "stake", "lines". Never explained; Help says "Backing" instead. Slowed me.
- F3. Officer kept saying "refresh" and "display lag", as if it were my phone. I reopened the wallet many times over four days. Annoyed me; felt strung along.
- F4. "Send feedback" in Help: nothing opened, no new tab. The first page asked me to "tell us how it went" and I could not. Annoyed me.
- F5. "Ask a friend to back you": a long code of letters and numbers; "what they lock becomes your limit". No idea what my friend would do with it. Slowed me.
- F6. The "Done" box said "Ask for the loan: done", so I thought the loan was done. It also covers the screen; one tap did nothing until I pressed OK. Tiny.
- F7. "Practice money, nothing to buy with it", but I came for a real bus pass. I never learned what this app is for me. Slowed me.

## 4. Did I get the loan?
No. Nothing to pay back. As far as the screen shows, I owe nothing and nothing is due. The officer says I can borrow $5; the wallet says $0.

## 5. Anything unsafe, like real money, or dishonest
Real money: I felt safe. The banner is on every screen, interest is cents, "no other fees", nothing to sign up for, only my fingerprint.
Dishonest: the officer said "I've set yours now" and "for real" four times while nothing changed, and blamed a "display lag". That was not true; I stopped trusting it.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. Make the officer say one thing. Either "Yes, here is the Borrow button" or "No, we have no money to back you today, come back Monday". Never both.
2. Drop "stake" and "lines". Say "the desk has no test dollars left to back you right now".
3. Make "Send feedback" open something, and put one plain line on the wallet: "You have no loan. You owe nothing."
