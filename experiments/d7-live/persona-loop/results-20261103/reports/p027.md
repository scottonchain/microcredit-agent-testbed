# Femi, 34, night security guard. Big text on my phone.

## 1. What I wanted
A small loan, about $5, for the deposit on a rented sewing machine, paid back in a few days. I was afraid the app would take real money.

## 2. What happened, step by step
- Opened the link. Yellow banner: "test tokens, not real money". Big, easy text (001-look.png).
- Tapped 'Get started'. "One moment…" with nothing to tap, then the wallet was ready. No fingerprint asked that I noticed.
- Picked '$5', 'Work tools', 'In a week'. Green box: get $5.00, about $0.01 interest in a week, $0.04 at most (006-tap_In_a_week.png).
- Tapped 'Ask for this loan'. The officer answered in about 15 seconds: yes, up to $5, plus a few cents for interest.
- I typed asking if real money or my bank would be touched. Answer: nothing real moves, no bank or card. That calmed me.
- Tapped 'Borrow $5.00 now'. Box: "done. You owe $5.00, due Dec 28, 2026" (012-tap_Borrow_5_00_now.png). Wallet $5.10.
- Opened 'More', then 'Help'. In Help I opened 'Done testing?' thinking it asked if I was finished. It showed 'Remove this wallet from this phone'. I tapped it. A box asked "Remove wallet from this phone?" (018-tap_Remove_this_wallet_from_th.png). I panicked and tapped 'Keep it'. Nothing lost.
- Put the phone away 3 days. Dec 1: owe "$5.00 plus 0.38 of a cent", due in 27 days (021-days_3.png).
- 'Pay back', then 'Pay back everything' (022-tap_Pay_back.png). Box: "Loan closed. Wallet now $0.10. You owe nothing."
- Sent feedback through 'Tell us how it went'. "Your note is with the team."

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1. Help, 'Done testing?'. I expected "are you finished?" or a survey. I saw a remove-wallet button and a scary box. I was sure my $5 and my loan would disappear. 'Keep it' saved me, but my heart was racing. Slowed me and scared me.
- F2. The remove box says "Nothing on the network is deleted" and "make sure you saved your recovery code". I do not know what "the network" is or what the code does to my loan. It did not say "your loan and dollars stay safe". Annoyed me / scary.
- F3. "0.38 of a cent of interest". With my eyes I first read 38 cents. And "You paid $5.00: $5.00 plus 0.38 of a cent" does not add up to me. Tiny but confusing.
- F4. The phone's date jumped from Nov 3 to Nov 28 while the wallet was being made. Tiny.
- F5. "Anyone on the test network can read this" next to "What is it for?". Who is anyone? Made me uneasy. Annoyed me.
- F6. After 'Get started', "One moment…" with nothing to tap. I did not know if it broke. Tiny.

## 4. Did I get the loan? Did I pay it back?
Yes: $5.00 of test dollars. Three days later I paid everything with one tap. The screen says the loan is closed, I owe nothing, $0.10 is left, no due date, and I can borrow up to $5.00 again.

## 5. Anything that felt unsafe, like real money, or dishonest
- Banner and officer both said clearly it is not real money; nothing asked for a card or bank. Good.
- 'Remove this wallet' one tap away in Help, under a friendly question, felt unsafe. Had I tapped 'Remove it' I would not know if I still owed $5 or how to get back in.
- "Public on the test network" is honest but I do not know who reads it.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. Hide the remove button better and call it what it is: "Delete this wallet from my phone (your loan stays)". Not under "Done testing?".
2. Say interest in plain cents: "less than 1 cent", and make the totals add up on screen.
3. Say in one line who "anyone on the test network" is, and show something while "One moment…" loads.
