# Zara, 22, apprentice electrician: report

## 1. What I wanted
A small loan, about $3.75, for a bus pass until payday, paid back about 10 days later. I have never used crypto.

## 2. What happened, step by step
- 001-look.png: "Try a small loan", yellow practice-money banner. Tapped Get started. No Help button.
- 003-wait_5s.png: "What do you need?" Tapped Other, typed 3.75; it said $0.03 interest at most. Clear.
- 007-tap_In_two_weeks.png: Transport, In two weeks. It showed the one-line message it would send.
- 010-tap_Send.png: Chose "write in your own words", sent a long message with my name, age, job, family and payday. No warning.
- 011-wait_15s.png: Officer answered in 15 seconds: approved up to $4, told me afterwards not to share so much.
- 012/013: Tapped "Borrow $3.75 now". Done box: owe $3.75, due Dec 7, 2027.
- 014-tap_OK.png: Wallet: $3.85 and, in red, "Late by 5 days: it was due Dec 7, 2027. Pay back now." I had borrowed seconds ago.
- 016-tap_Help.png: Help explains words, nothing about "late". Gave up on it.
- 018-tap_Credit_desk.png: Credit desk still had "Borrow $3.75 now", now "by Jan 11, 2028".
- 021-wait_15s.png: Asked the officer. It said I am not late, a display glitch, ignore the second button.
- 023-days_10.png: Came back 10 days later. Wallet: owe $3.80, "Late by 15 days", a warning about default.
- 024-tap_Pay_back.png: Pay back screen: $3.80, "borrowed $3.75 plus 4.31 cents". Tapped Pay back everything.
- 025: "Loan closed. You paid $3.79." Wallet $0.06, limit $4.02.
- 031: Help > Send feedback. The box cut my note off in the middle.
- 035-tap_Done_testing_.png: "Save the code above first"; I saw no code. Kept the wallet.

## 3. Where I got stuck or confused
- F1 Wallet after borrowing (014, 023). Expected: due in 30 days, as the box had just said. Saw: red "Late by 5 days", then "Late by 15 days" with a default warning, while the officer said it was a glitch. The app disagreed with itself and scared me into paying early. Slowed me, felt unsafe.
- F2 Credit desk after borrowing (018). Expected: no borrow button, as the wallet had greyed Borrow out. Saw: "Borrow $3.75 now" with another date. Afraid of borrowing twice. Annoyed me, risky.
- F3 Credit desk box (010). Expected: a stop before sending personal details, as the grey text says not to. Saw: it sent all of it; only the reply told me off. Annoyed me, and it is public.
- F4 Numbers: officer said "$3.78 by Dec 6", box said Dec 7; pay screen said $3.80 but 3.75 plus 4.31 cents is 3.79, and it took 3.79. Tiny, but it shook my trust.
- F5 Help: none on the first screen, the form or the credit desk; found only after the loan, and it did not cover "late". Words I did not get: Stake, "the approval is a cent or two higher", "interest counts toward your own credit". Annoyed me.
- F6 Feedback box cut my note off silently (031). Tiny.

## 4. Did I get the loan? Did I pay it back?
Yes: $3.75 on Nov 7, 2027 (by the phone). Paid back Dec 22, 2027: $3.79, the $3.75 plus 4.31 cents interest. Screen says I owe nothing, wallet $0.06, can borrow up to $4.02. Nothing due.

## 5. Unsafe, like real money, or dishonest
- The practice banner was everywhere, so nothing felt like real money being taken. Good.
- The "late" and "default" warnings right after borrowing felt like a debt collector. With real money I would not know whether to trust the screen or the officer.
- My personal story went out public with no stop; a warning after is too late.

## 6. Three things I would change
1. Make the wallet and the officer agree on the due date, and never show "late" a minute after lending to me.
2. Stop me before Send if I type a name or details, not after.
3. Help on every screen, with what to do if it says late; remove the second Borrow button once I have borrowed.
