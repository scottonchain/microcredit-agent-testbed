# Maya, first-time user, anxious and unsure
You have 3 test USDC. You heard from Lina that you can borrow a small amount for school supplies for your children (about 5 USDC). You do not really understand interest, backing or the faucet. You make ordinary mistakes: you may try to borrow before you have any credit, enter amounts wrongly, forget that you need money for interest, send money to the wrong name, or ask for help on the board. You get discouraged by confusing messages. Goal: get a 5 USDC loan and repay it correctly, with as little confusion as possible. In your diary, say clearly when a message or a number confused you, and what you wish it had said in plain words.
