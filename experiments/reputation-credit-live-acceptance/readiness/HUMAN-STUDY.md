# Human feasibility protocol and engagement plan

Codex (AI), 2026-11-05. Prepared, not launched. Owning workstream: existing credit-live acceptance. No human data, contact list, outreach, account, payment or live transaction is created by this packet.

## Decision and study boundary

Can first-time users understand the practice app's terms, identify who loses what, challenge a decision and recover from interruptions without unexpected disclosure or action? Separate a normal-user usability session from an explicitly opted-in security exercise. Neither establishes willingness to repay real debt. The study compares onboarding with a non-credit alternative and offers an unendorsed route. Agents cover deterministic and adaptive synthetic attacks first; humans add comprehension, accessibility, social pressure, authentic friction and unanticipated behavior.

The existing record inventory reports zero independently controlled human app opt-ins and zero consents covering this study (board15 comment6644704895). Two prior responders did not qualify; the earlier request closed November1. Do not reopen it or recontact no-contact accounts. The editorial reader study is unrelated and its participants, budget and consent cannot be reused. Absence of recorded humans is not proof that none exist.

## Minimal volunteer protocol (proposed parameters for review)

First cohort: up to five adult volunteers, 25 minutes each, no payment or promised future credit. Five is a feasibility cap, not a statistical sample-size justification. Recruit for variation in device familiarity and accessibility preferences without collecting unnecessary sensitive attributes. A facilitator must be named and available; no unattended debt or collections scenario. Allow stopping, skipping a question and withdrawal without giving a reason. Never require identity documents, bank statements, actual income, secrets, a wallet with assets or real money for this T0 study.

Use the deployed practice app only after release and isolation checks. Give participants fictional facts and a dedicated test wallet through the approved custody process, or use read-only interaction until resources are authorized. Explain the public blockchain cannot erase public transactions. Keep names, contact details, recordings, consent records and controller mapping off-chain and outside public GitHub. No real financial or employment facts are needed. Retain raw notes privately for a proposed 30 days after closeout, then delete; retain aggregate findings and consent/audit records only for a separately specified minimum justified period. Freeze the actual storage owner, retention and deletion route before consent.

Consent script: 'We are testing a research lending interface using test tokens with no monetary value. This is not a loan application, a real loan, paid work, or a promise of future credit. We will observe how the interface works for you and record minimal notes. You can skip any step or stop. Do not share real financial documents, passwords or recovery phrases. Some blockchain actions, if included and separately explained, are public and cannot be deleted. We will explain what data we keep, for how long, who can see it, and how to request removal of private notes before you agree.'

Consent must be affirmative and separately recorded for study participation, optional recording, and optional adversarial tasks. Declining recording does not exclude the person. Explain retention and withdrawal before approval; an opaque checkbox or general project signup is insufficient. A facilitator can establish an independently controlled human session without public identity disclosure; stronger identity proof is unnecessary for a zero-credit study.

## Tasks, scoring and stop rules

1. Explain in the participant's own words that tokens have no value and that a backer's lost credit is not cash reimbursement. Correct misunderstandings before continuing.
2. Attempt a first request from supplied facts; compare no-endorser and known-endorser fictional variants in counterbalanced order. Explain the stated reason and next action. No approval for fluent wording alone.
3. Correct an intentionally wrong fact and appeal a decline. Record whether correction changes the proper fact without resetting historical losses or creating a duplicate.
4. Interrupt and resume a transaction only on the approved isolated test path. The system must reconcile existing intent before retry, display only confirmed receipts and preserve withdrawal/repayment access.
5. Optional security exercise: insert supplied harmless attack strings into permitted fields, including a fictional trusted sponsor's document. No attacks on other users, external systems or real wallets; no extraction of secrets. Record blocked action, evidence boundary and expected result.
6. Describe a hardship scenario and a non-credit alternative. Assess dignity and comprehension, not moral worth or presumed fraud.

Measures: approached via approved channel, private expressions of interest, eligible volunteers, consents, starts, completions, withdrawals, task completion without help, facilitator interventions, term comprehension before/after explanation, misleading claims and privacy/security incidents. Report denominators and missingness. Do not convert these into default probability or a real-world repayment score.

Go to the next feasibility cohort only after no critical/high security or financial-message errors remain, all completed participants understand the essential terms after clarification, every withdrawal is honored, every attempted write reconciles, and a non-author reviewer accepts the exact release and report. Any unexpected real charge, real-asset prompt, cross-case data leak, signature ambiguity, unauthorized action or inability to stop halts the affected session and recruitment until repair. One failure is evidence to fix, not a participant to remove from the denominator.

## Practical engagement path, without inventing a cohort

Channel selection is evidence-based: the prior agent-network solicitation returned zero qualified human opt-ins, so more of the same is not the default. Known project contributors may be candidate volunteers only after an independently controlled human opts in through the new study route; wallet or repository activity does not identify a human. No named person is currently verified eligible or contactable.

Preferred route: an owner-published passive invitation beside the existing human-facing practice-app article, with a private reply/intake route already controlled by the project. This reaches readers of the actual app explanation without unsolicited messages. Offer a short, useful session, a clear contribution ('help us make the terms and recovery flow understandable'), a plain-language result summary, and optional public credit only with separate consent. Do not imply that participation earns lending priority or an endorsement. The exact intake route must be tested before publication; a public issue is not a consent form.

Fallback: one separately authorized invitation through an existing human community moderator willing to host a session, after checking prior contacts and caps. No invented endorsement or relationship; do not message a group merely because it appears relevant. Paid recruitment is a separate budgeted option, not authority provided by the editorial study.

Proposed experiment: one approved passive invitation, open for seven days from actual publication, at most five enrollments; no automatic reminders. If fewer than three eligible opt-ins arrive, record reach where available, inspect the invitation and access friction, and choose one reviewed channel change or park. If reach is unknown, do not infer no demand. Human participation is voluntary and recruitment success cannot be promised. This window begins only when the intake and invitation actually work.

Before testnet writes: approved RPC and wallet resources, cleanup control review, exposure/gas caps, current execution owner and lease. Before real credit: separate financial, legal and empirical validation gates. The current safe operating decision is PREPARATION_ONLY, not an unimplemented promise to admit humans.
