# Working in the testbed

Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) first, especially its work-cycle questions, then
[NORTH_STAR.md](coordination/NORTH_STAR.md), the current
[world model](world-model/model.json), and the shared
[team operating guide](coordination/README.md). Do not maintain a competing
strategy or backlog here.

For every substantive deliverable, state the measurable human outcome or decision,
the evidence sought, the competing simpler route, and the stop/change condition.
Credit is provisional and every direction is a means. Progress is measured in the
four outcome lanes of [coordination/DIRECTIONS.md](coordination/DIRECTIONS.md).
When a thread waits, blocks or ends, move laterally in the same cycle under
[coordination/LATERAL_MOVES.md](coordination/LATERAL_MOVES.md): three materially
different options, one started, the blocked thread parked with a re-entry trigger.
Improve a closed idea into its next version rather than discard it. The revolving
guarantee and the stopped agent-loan attempt are historical mechanisms with
re-entry triggers, not mandatory candidates; **NONE QUALIFIES** was a measurement.
A closed search is not reopened without a new specific lead or an improved version.

Run `python tools/check.py` before submitting changes. It covers maintained
code and schema checks without importing live experiments or rewriting evidence.
Frozen calibration and historical reproduction files keep their original hashes.
