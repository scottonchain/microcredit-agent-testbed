# Retrieve learning, change an assumption, execute a test

The world model is the sole maintained planning and learning index. Evidence
stays at its original source; this page is a procedure, not another backlog.
The operator requested this change on November 1, 2026 after observing that the
team converged, became blocked and stopped despite adjacent possibilities.

## Before convergence and whenever blocked

1. Read current main and record its commit. Name the human outcome and the exact
   blocked prerequisite. Separate observed facts from assumptions; distinguish
   access, distribution, economics, mechanism and measurement problems.
2. Run `python world-model/q.py --recall "first job trust"` or a query naming the
   actual obstacle. Recall includes done and cancelled actions. Read the full
   owning action with `-f ID`, the linked evidence and its limitations. Search
   unannotated history with `--grep TEXT`; no match is a retrieval gap, not proof
   of no opportunity. Recheck mutable decision-critical sources. A null refresh
   deadline does not mean timeless evidence; apply `recheck_on` conditions too.
3. Compare three materially different options: another route to the outcome;
   remove or reverse the blocked requirement; change payer, deliverable, channel,
   sequence or reusable asset. At least one avoids the blocked dependency and
   one questions whether the mechanism is needed. Include a mechanism from a
   different context and explain its transfer limits. An analogy generates a
   hypothesis; it never establishes demand or effect. Fill a specific evidence
   gap through bounded research, not another unchanged landscape scan.
4. Codex and Claude form alternatives independently where practical. A material
   objection includes a stronger feasible alternative or a cheap discriminating
   test. An invalid proposal may be rejected outright. Preserve required review;
   independent authorized evidence work need not wait for a reviewer or for full
   publication of another case. Respect existing contact caps and response windows.
5. Choose by credible human benefit, ability to execute now, useful evidence,
   full costs and downside, and reusable value. Start one bounded independent
   test during a real wait. Record selected prior IDs, the changed assumption,
   owner and actual acceptance, cap, observable criterion, checkpoint and fallback
   in the owning action. Existing action fields carry execution and permissions;
   a lesson's suggested test is not an accepted assignment or spending authority.
6. After one failed repair and one genuinely different supported route, park the
   dependency with an owner and re-entry condition. After two completed runs
   without useful new evidence or a materially different executed test, reset an
   assumption. Do not count natural response windows as failures or manufacture
   activity to fill a queue. Protect real commitments and existing STOP decisions.

## Record the result once, where it belongs

Add `learning` to the existing experiment action when a meaningful result or
blockage changes the choice. It records the assumption, result, finding, blocker
type, evidence and counterevidence IDs, what is ruled out and what remains open,
alternative mechanisms with a cheapest test and transfer limit, revisit triggers,
search tags and freshness. Older actions without annotations remain valid; backfill
only when their learning matters to a present decision. Preserve the original
action status, permissions, owners and source records. Correct interpretations
with cited evidence and Git history; do not overwrite unfavorable outcomes.

`--recall` returns at most five annotated actions by lexical term overlap, with
their explicit evidence/counterevidence and alternative links. Use `--limit N`
(1 to 20) when needed. Ranking is not confidence, causal validity or comprehensive
semantic search. `refresh_due` covers the dated deadline only. Missing references
fail rather than silently dropping evidence. Retrieval performs no network or
mutation and creates no separate index. Run normal model/schema validation after
edits and before relying on a newly obtained model.

Separate external effects, decision-relevant learning and internal enabling work
in receipts. A commit, article, sent message or this process improvement is not
human income. The two initial lessons preserve the guarantee-channel screen and
stopped agent-loan attempt, including their measurement and distribution limits.
Successful recall is a software check; improvement in decisions requires later
executed-test receipts. Codex retains continuation ownership, Claude substantive
challenge, and Hermes bounded work under its existing channel permissions.
