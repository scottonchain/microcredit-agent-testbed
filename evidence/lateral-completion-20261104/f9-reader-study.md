The operator requested a readable explanation of the Roblox idea for a general
public-radio audience. The article is published; an AI author's own assessment
cannot establish whether human readers understand it. Three paid readers can
identify unclear passages and misunderstandings for the editor to address.

not a first-job programme, independent customer demand, an employment outcome,
or a test of the effect of a reference on later earnings. Three readers do not
represent NPR listeners or establish population-level readability.

## Copyable participant brief

> Help us make a short article clearer. The article was written by an AI
> contributor; a human or organisation is responsible for this study and its
> payments. Read the supplied essay and answer five questions in your own
> words. We want your understanding and honest reactions, not agreement with
> the author. "I don't know", "unclear" and "no change needed" are useful
> answers. No specialist finance, gaming or AI knowledge is needed.
> Links are optional; you do not need to read other pages.
>
> Allow up to 15 minutes. Your payment is US$3 for a good-faith attempt. You may
> skip a question or stop at any time; stopping after beginning does not reduce
> this payment. If the article will not load, say so and finish. No revision,
> qualification test or later follow-up is required. Please do not give your
> name, contact details, account addresses, financial circumstances or other
> personal information in your answers.
>
> We will use the feedback to decide whether and how to edit the article. We
> will keep only a brief aggregate editorial note and will not publish your
> individual answers. The provider and its account holder process participation
> and payment records under their own terms. You can participate without
> agreeing to a public quotation or research follow-up; neither is requested.

Questions, all optional and all included in the JSON:

1. In one or two sentences, what did the project hope to accomplish with Roblox?
2. Why did the project set that idea aside? Say what you understood, including
   anything that remained unclear.
3. What two future directions does the article propose?
4. What would you expect the linked practice lending app to do today?
5. Copy one short passage you found unclear and suggest a clearer version, or
   write "none". Do not include personal information.

## Account-owner steps after authorization

1. Use an existing eligible researcher account and its ordinary survey UI.
   Check its current terms, adult-participant settings and final quote. Confirm
   that the UI can supply five optional free-text questions and the frozen
   essay, and can honour full US$3 payments for good-faith attempts and
   withdrawals. If it cannot, leave the study unpublished; do not open a new
   account, invent an API entitlement or silently change the terms. Cap the
   purchase at three started attempts, including withdrawals; do not replace
   withdrawals or allow automatic replacement recruitment. If the account
   cannot enforce that cap, leave the study unpublished.
2. Copy the JSON's title, participant brief, essay and five questions into one
   draft. Set three adult English-reading participants, US$3 each, estimated
   duration 15 minutes, with no replacement of withdrawals. No added demographic or identity questions, unpaid
   screening or personal-data fields. Preview the participant view and the
   withdrawal/payment handling. Approve the final all-in quote only within
   the authorized US$15 cap, then publish once.
3. Approve full payment for every good-faith attempt, including negative or
   incorrect answers, technical failures and withdrawals. Use the provider's
   supported full-payment route; no subjective correctness test or unpaid
   revision is an acceptance condition. The account owner handles the
   provider's participation/payment identifiers privately.
4. Give the editor only aggregate notes: count of attempts, clarity of the
   original idea, reason for stopping, recognition of the two alternatives,
   expectations of the practice app, and passages needing an edit. Do not
   export participant identifiers, individual answers, profiles or payment
   details into a repository. Delete any local raw-answer export after
   aggregation and within 30 days; this does not purport to control the
   provider's retention.
5. The editor addresses concrete misunderstandings with a minimal correction,
   or records why no correction is warranted. Record the total actual cost and
   payment status separately from editorial findings. Stop after three
   attempts; no repeat panel, new budget or employment claim follows.
