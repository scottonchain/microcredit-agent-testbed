## Exact budget test before claiming cheap credit

Use separate quantities: cycle default incidence p; loss given default after recoveries LGD; attributable per-loan servicing/ramp/gas/collection cost C; collectible interest I. For a USD25 advance, expected principal loss is `25*p*LGD`, not p dollars. A generous necessary condition, assigning **all** gross interest to loss and service, is `C + 25*p*LGD <= I`; real budget must also allocate lender return, protocol fees, reserve retention, capital/time costs and any taxes. Sponsor absorption is a disclosed transfer of loss, not elimination of cost.

At EFFR433bps plus premium1400bps, APR1833bps,30 elapsed days, the contract's simple-interest arithmetic gives I≈USD0.3766438356 (actual token arithmetic rounds to micro-USDC). With zero recovery, p=3% gives USD0.75 expected loss and p=6% gives USD1.50. They already exceed gross interest by USD0.373356 and USD1.123356 **before servicing**. With C=0, recoveries must exceed49.7808% or74.8904% respectively; any nonzero cost increases those thresholds. With full irrevocable collateral, quantify the sponsor's loss and opportunity cost separately and prove actual collateral allocation.

The generous gross-interest upper bound is1.506575% per30-day cycle. If default also forfeits interest, the stronger exact condition is `(1−p)*I − p*25 − C >= 0`, giving `p <= (I−C)/(25+I)`; at C=0 and actual micro-USDC interest0.376643, p≤1.48421129% per cycle. An assumed10% annual incidence becomes0.862238% in30 days only under a stationary constant-hazard model (`1−0.9^(30/365)`); it is not comparable to3–6% observed per-cycle loss. On that artificial model, expected principal loss isUSD0.215560 and gross residual isUSD0.161084 before all other allocations. First borrowers, seasonality and selection invalidate automatic conversion.

For illustration only, allocating annual operating expense proportional to a constant USD25 balance gives USD0.390411 at19% and USD0.575342 at28% over30 days—already above gross interest. This is **not** an estimate of actual cost per pilot loan. Agent automation would have to reduce measured local cost, or the product needs explicit sponsorship/recovery/different terms; quoting cheap Base transactions does not demonstrate either.

## Minimum primary evidence needed from a real partner

One cohort export must identify unique people/controllers, first versus repeat loans, principal and denomination, origination/due dates, status at fixed days past due, principal repaid, accrued/collected interest, fees, write-off policy/date, recoveries after write-off, and collateral/sponsor payments. Report loan-count default, principal-weighted final loss, recovery fraction and confidence intervals separately. PAR30 is a point-in-time outstanding portfolio delinquency stock; write-off is an accounting flow; repayment count is not an amount-loss rate. None substitutes for a matured30-day origination cohort.

One servicing-cost time sheet and cost ledger must record onboarding/KYC, assessment, disbursement, borrower assistance, normal collections, late cases, appeals, reconciliations and setup amortization. Multiply measured time by paid staff/agent/provider rates; include agent inference, human escalation, partner overhead, ramps, cash handling, FX/spreads and actual gas. Separate first-loan fixed costs from repeat servicing and disclose donated labor/software/project subsidies. Request the partner's actual USD25-sized product, not a portfolio average across USD500 loans.

Precommit reserve/stop budget before borrowing. Do not call the pilot calibrated from20–50 people: zero defaults in50 independent matured loans still has an approximate one-sided95% upper incidence bound5.82% (`1−0.05^(1/50)`), even before correlated group failures or imperfect follow-up. Group risk is correlated; the binomial bound is optimistic. A “3–6%” row can be a sensitivity scenario; it cannot be called observed, predicted or a proven automation benefit.

**Decision:** keep3–6% only as labeled cycle-sensitivity inputs with explicit LGD/subsidy. Keep servicing cost unknown until measured. Do not blend annual calibration defaults, annual operating ratios, PAR30, platform repayment and cycle losses into one economic claim. These unknowns block an unsubsidized human-loan profitability claim, not honest planning of a small explicitly loss-budgeted pilot.
