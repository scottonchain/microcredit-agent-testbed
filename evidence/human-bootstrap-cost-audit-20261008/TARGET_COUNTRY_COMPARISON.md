# Target countries: a practical bootstrap screen

Codex (AI), 8 October 2026. This updates the comparison for Claude's redirected
assignment. Guatemala is the first research priority; Ethiopia remains
conditional. **No country or partner is qualified for a human pilot yet.**

| Country | Lender and group model | Payment route and current obstacle |
|---|---|---|
| Guatemala | Decree 25-2016 distinguishes authorized commercial microfinance entities from nonprofit registration. Its nonprofit rule forbids third-party deposits and debt issuance; counsel must determine whether the proposed pool funding fits. Friendship Bridge's own 2025 reports are indexed, but their full documents were
inaccessible: current loss, recovery and servicing figures are unverified.
Its published monthly rate is not a comparable effective APR or a $25 offer.
CGAP's cited default improvement is a lab game; annual portfolio expense,
arrears, final loss and cycle default must remain separate measures.

The shared economic gate is concrete: a 25-USDC, 30-day loan at 18.33% total APR
earns 0.376643 USDC. Hypothetical 3–6% full-loss cycle incidence exceeds that
interest before operating costs. Sponsor stake assigns losses to risk capital;
it does not erase them. Loss-support figures assume all interest is available
and therefore do not prove the configured reserve sufficient.

The path into a human economy is to measure the agent workflow first, then
cost an existing local group's productive working-capital loan with a lawful
lender and an operating payment route. Compare prepayment and local-currency
funding before choosing USDC credit. Verify a useful net payment and repayment
from actual sale proceeds; fund any finite subsidy explicitly. Expand only
after observed costs, matured losses and secured trust capacity fit the budget.
No outreach, borrowing, funds or new schedule were committed by this research.
