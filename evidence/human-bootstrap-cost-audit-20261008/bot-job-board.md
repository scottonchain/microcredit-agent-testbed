## Do not manufacture a working-capital gap

A bot can perform these offline/read-only checks without holding ETH. Publishing evidence may require an authorized team channel, not a financed chain write. Registering an identity, buying an optional API, borrowing to repay, or choosing a paid input where the free one meets acceptance does not establish productive need. A blocked credential/channel is not automatically a cash shortage.

For any proposed paid variant, require a specific mandatory input invoice and why supplied/free alternatives fail acceptance. Then calculate gap as necessary upfront cash minus spendable borrower cash, buyer prepayment and direct sponsorship. Prefer buyer prepayment/direct input payment when it removes the gap at lower risk/cost. Scope principal to the residual gap, never to a round-number5USDC merely because the demo specifies5.

Claude's zero-ETH/paymaster5-USDC run can test routing, third-party repayment and accounting. Until a necessary paid input is demonstrated, describe it as a subsidized product-mechanics test rather than an economically admitted loan. If optional registrations/chain calls are part of a requested experiment, disclose that the team created both the expenditure and payment. Gas costs/paymaster capability require fresh measurements; this draft does not assume a testnet Circle route works.

## Payment and capital provenance

Any eventual team's USDC payment is internal employer funding, even if it passes through a different address or agent. An independent customer requires independent control, actual accepted output and customer-funded payment receipts. Employment demand, productive value and financing necessity are separate claims.

The original recoverable15-USDC simulation seed and existing staged/source-restoration obligations remain ringfenced. They fund no job purchases, retained dues, intentional defaults or new employer compensation here. Ten completed payments do not make internal funding external revenue, and a same-controller agent does not satisfy an independent-operator gate.
