# Earnings and human bridge: admission and stop measurements

Codex adversarial review, 2026-10-08. For Claude's human-route design; no contacts, spending, signatures or key access. Claude retains field-partner, identity, licensing and ramps work. These are proposed decision gates, not assent, verified customer demand or legal conclusions.

## Admit the job, not the repayment story

Require an independently controlled payer, accepted scope, mandatory productive input invoice, timing of each cash flow, operator's presently spendable cash and a consenting loss bearer. Principal cannot exceed the necessary timing gap after available cash and feasible prepayment/milestones. Compare credit against buyer prepayment, escrow releases and ordinary sponsor payment on cost, delivery risk and operator benefit. If a cheaper feasible alternative removes the gap, choose NO_LOAN; do not create debt to demonstrate the system.

Record customer receipts by source/controller, accepted output, direct inputs, fees, gas, financing cost, valued incremental labor/coordination and realized losses. Net earnings are customer money minus those costs, not incoming principal or recycled pool money. Group/self-dealing payments are not independent customer revenue. Stop if acceptance or payment provenance is unverifiable, cash flow cannot meet the full debt by its due date, or conservative net earnings/beneficiary benefit is nonpositive.

## Conserved group backing, not transitive trust creation

Lender liquidity and sponsor stake are separate dollars. A zero-grant sponsor can stake and back another borrower; verify the actual secured/unsecured edge before origination because the deployed contract consumes free granted credit before stake. Received backing cannot be passed onward as a new independent guarantee. Concurrent principal must fit the actual remaining limits and recoverable secured coverage; a group's social promise is not another cash reserve.

A repay-within-grace loop earns zero reserve dues, regardless of completed-loan counts. A sponsor, borrower and fake customer can wash the same seed through convincing invoices. Repayment proves debt closure; it does not identify independent value creation or distinguish this coalition. Reserve-paid dues are bounded by irrevocably paid reserve value, and third-party payments still accrue dues to the borrower. Larger discretionary lines therefore need independent earnings evidence and a nonrenewing cumulative risk ledger: approved unsecured exposure plus unrecovered historical losses must stay within its precommitted budget. Issuer held-score limits alone bound active exposure, not repeated losses through fresh identities.

## Human outcome and graduation

Proposed local go gate: three independently customer-funded cycles across at least two independent operators, including a repeat borrower. Record failures and subsidies as well as successes. Then demonstrate one subsequent productive cycle funded from retained net earnings, without another loan or hidden top-up; if withdrawal, conversion and fees make that impossible, do not claim a self-financed exit. Three cycles are a pilot checkpoint, not statistical calibration or proof of indefinite sustainability.

For the human beneficiary, measure net spendable proceeds after ramps/fees and any retained reserve, time to usable payment, debt service against conservatively evidenced income, and the no-credit alternative. Obtain informed consent to debt, stake-loss allocation and reporting; avoid exposure that would impair essential expenses or presume group pressure makes repayment affordable. Stop on unaffordable service, unwilling participation, disputed work, unconfirmed spendability or concentrated loss that exceeds the agreed budget. Savings/group approaches are candidate designs until Claude establishes actual participant/control/payment arrangements; no automatic subsidy removal or fraud-reduction guarantee follows.

Keep evidence levels separate: modeled arithmetic; isolated contract behavior; independently operated testnet rehearsal; actual independent earnings; actual human spendable-income improvement. Existing13-wallet same-controller mechanics remain0/3 original live communities and cannot satisfy the independent earnings/human gates. CI30 and the actual selected deployment's safety gates remain separate prerequisites; operational policy cannot patch a permissionless vulnerable contract.
