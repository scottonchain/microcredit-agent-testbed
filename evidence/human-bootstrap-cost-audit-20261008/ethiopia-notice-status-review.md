# Ethiopia source-status check

Codex, 2026-10-08. Bounded documentary check, not legal advice or clearance.

- **Primary verified indexed notice:** NBE's [27 February2026 Birr-paired P2P notice](https://nbe.gov.et/nbe_news/public-notice-notice-on-illegal-birr-paired-peer-to-peer-p2p-transactions-via-trading-platforms/) says Birr-paired P2P arrangements and cryptocurrency exchange are not permitted without explicit NBE authorization. Canonical primary page content was available through connected-web search; open timed out. This is a notice of existing restrictions, not a newly enacted digital-lending law.
- **July23 broader notice remains primary-artifact unverified in this pass:** news articles and a Telegram account reproduce a broader virtual-asset notice dated23 July2026, but no official NBE July23 page/PDF was retrieved. The Telegram account's NBE ownership has not been established. Retain the reported notice as attributed secondary evidence; do not silently upgrade it to authenticated primary text or read its exception as authorization for this project.
- **Primary draft, not permission:** NBE hosts [National Digital Payments Strategy2026–2030 draft](https://nbe.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/NATIONAL_DIGITAL_PAYMENT_STRATEGY_2026-2030_Draft_Document.pdf). Indexed text expressly labels it DRAFT and lists a digital-lending directive as not implemented, with development/implementation as a future24-month target. NBE's [NDPS page](https://nbe.gov.et/ndps/) likewise calls the strategy an official draft. A strategy target is neither an enacted lender licence nor authorization for stablecoin lending.
- **Specific new digital-lender bill not verified:** this bounded search found no authenticated enacted instrument or exact proposed bill text establishing a new stand-alone digital-lender licensing route. This does not prove no bill exists. Require exact title, bill/directive number, regulator/parliament text, enactment/effective date and applicability before describing it as law. Broader banking legislation and plans to develop digital-credit rules must stay separate.

Operational inference: Ethiopia is not an evidenced ready USDC/cash-out pilot route here. Obtain the exact July23 primary artifact and an applicable authorized lender/asset/payment route through qualified review before treating it as eligible. Do not generalize a digital-payments draft into a virtual-asset exemption.
