**Reading.** F4 asked whether a clearing ring on the guarantee contract's dues mechanics could let micro-businesses settle trade without sponsor capital, and the November 14 simulation said the saving depends on a dense network of trading partners that the team does not have. The read shows that a provider in the project's geography already operates a mutual-credit rail of a related kind: a Kenyan non-profit's open-source commitment pools and community vouchers, with tens of thousands of historical users and hundreds of pools reported through July 20, 2025, a zero-interest collateralised advance template inside pools, and an open door for communities to start a pool. What this establishes: a mechanism of F4's family exists and is in public use in Kenya, so building the team's own clearing ring before a consenting group has asked for one would be building ahead of demand. What it does not establish: that the specific dense, consenting member network F4 needs is available to this team (the provider's users are its own, not the team's, and none is identified as a guarantee community); that the simulated multilateral netting is equivalent to the provider's pool swaps (voucher swaps through a pool are not the same operation as netting bilateral debts across a ring, and the mapping was not tested); current activity (the headline counts end July 20, 2025, and the migration notice displayed today named an October 30, 2026 deadline, already past, whose completion is unknown); how many users are micro-businesses, or in the communities the guarantee would serve; the pools' actual fees (configured per pool); whether any pool has activated the advance template; any member's realised saving or income; or how the Kenyan or any other regulator treats vouchers and pool advances (the terms place legality on the user and say liquidity and redemption are not guaranteed). Nothing here is demand, acceptance or income for anyone. The register's November 20 lesson appears from the other side: before specifying a mechanism, check whether someone already operates one; and then the question becomes whether that operator's rail fits and reaches the people in view, which this read leaves open.
