## Changed research choice

Make the unit of analysis a **fulfilled essential good or service**, with recipient value, supplier cost, delays and displaced alternatives—not an account, voucher or payment. The constructive Grassroots-inspired hypothesis is that credible unused local capacity plus unmet reciprocal needs can produce additional useful exchange. The competing hypothesis is that national-currency purchasing power or real supply is binding; a new clearing mechanism then adds little or causes harm.

For a future evidenced community, compare the same agreed service using a simple non-blockchain ledger/current exchange with a commitment-pool arrangement; preserve a cash/existing-service benchmark. Measure completed delivery and unmet need, all parties' time/input costs, disputes, cash displacement and net usable household resources. Neither token face value nor a supplier receipt alone establishes beneficiary value or causal additionality. No community, task, costed protocol or delivery owner is currently available to this project.

Comingle remains a redistribution/risk-sharing model and GoodDollar a funding/distribution model in the [completed global report](https://github.com/scottonchain/microcredit-agent-testbed/blob/cd844994ff96205e7e587e4618beaa7df4abdc09/evidence/global-poverty-research-20261023/README.md); neither supplies proof of new real resources by itself. Redistribution or risk-sharing can still improve welfare without increasing aggregate earnings; assess net receipts and volatility rather than rejecting that mechanism on accounting alone. This choice does not rank their current operators or reopen enrollment. Microloans and first payments remain tools only where an evidenced working-capital or first-exchange constraint exists; no loan, token or payment is proposed.

### Prospective evidence dimensions, not a benefit ladder

- **Protocol activity:** documented successful events, units and duplication rules. Does not establish receipt or usable resources.
- **Delivery:** identify what was received, when, and by whom in the permitted evaluation unit; distinguish issuer claim, recipient report and corroboration. Record the verifier's role, incentives and relationship to issuer/supplier/recipient. A populated status field is not proof of independence; recipient reports can have reporting error.
- **Incremental usable resources:** evidence relative to an explicit counterfactual, subtracting access costs, displacement and harms over a stated horizon. Activity or receipt alone does not enter this ledger. A transfer can count as a resource when supported, but not as earned income.
- **Incremental earnings:** evidenced net labor/business earnings relative to what otherwise would occur, with costs and displaced work; preserve the existing sponsor scorecard definition. No live scorecard/schema change is made here.
- **Welfare:** separately measured unmet essentials, volatility, health or other relevant outcomes. Not all welfare is a resource-dollar measure; do not force it into R or sum overlapping resource, earnings and welfare changes.

Coverage, missingness, measurement validity, comparison design, full costs and time horizon apply to every outcome claim. Evidence dimensions can remain unknown independently; stronger delivery provenance does not automatically upgrade causal inference. On the current v2 metadata read, there is no basis to assign a release-wide delivery level to unaudited reports.
