## Decision rules

1. Verify assigned deliverable, price, payer, acceptance, completion and actual
   available payment. A listing or testimonial alone is not an order receipt.
2. Reconstruct dated cash flows before financing. Calculate the maximum cash
   shortfall after existing unrestricted cash and customer deposits. A delay
   matters only if it blocks or reduces otherwise worthwhile work.
3. Compare the same order with existing resources, customer prepayment, ordinary
   platform payout options and a bounded advance. Prefer NO_LOAN when it wins.
4. Establish additionality: what would the business and worker have done without
   the advance? Record the participant's answer and supporting records, not an
   invented control group. A single case cannot establish causal portfolio impact.
5. Verify the worker or owner actually receives spendable proceeds and retains
   the agreed business rights. Compare net hourly earnings with the participant's
   stated acceptable floor and feasible alternative, including selling, setup,
   learning, administration and rework time.
6. Account for every pool cost, principal loss, refund, unpaid human contribution,
   top-up and capital-lock cost. Returned principal is not revenue or pool growth.

The operator is willing to consider small USDC contributions, but no amount,
network, destination or transaction has been specified or executed. Such capital
would have to return and support additional human income with a path to growth
after costs. The agency is a possible evidence partner, never a presumed grant
recipient. Passing money onward to a socially oriented organisation alone fails
the objective. Compare lateral alternatives such as direct customer prepayment,
shared tool access, faster settlement or a profitable service that removes the
measured bottleneck. Do not force a credit mechanism or USDC payment rail.

`order-template.json` names the missing measurements. `economics.py` refuses to
calculate a success result from missing values. Its synthetic tests check
accounting only and are never participant evidence. Use actual-source references
for every filled field, retain original currencies and date-specific FX evidence.

Business operating cash and human income are separate ledgers. Agency margin
does not establish money received by a worker or owner. The human ledger requires
an explicit participant boundary, actual customer-funded earned payments, that
participant's unreimbursed costs and finance fees, and all of their work hours.
An unknown distribution leaves the human outcome incomplete; a zero distribution
fails the positive-income criterion. Do not sum wages as both business surplus
and worker benefit. Loan proceeds and forgiven debt are excluded from earnings.
Even three positive accounting gates are not proof of causal or lasting benefit.
