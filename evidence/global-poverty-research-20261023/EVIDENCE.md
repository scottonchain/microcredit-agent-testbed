# Evidence map and audit boundaries

Research cut: **23 October 2026**. Links in the report are the claim-level references. This ledger records how to read them, rather than repeating their findings. It is not a systematic-review search log or a downloaded archive of copyrighted sources.

## Evidence classes

- **Population measurement:** household surveys, harmonization and model-based estimates. A publication year is not the year every person was surveyed.
- **Causal evaluation:** a comparison designed to identify an intervention's effect. Applicability depends on population, baseline, implementation and follow-up.
- **Operational report:** the implementing organization's account, funding, membership or incident data. Useful for understanding a model; not independent impact proof.
- **Protocol/documentation:** intended or supported functionality. Not proof of local availability, compliance or beneficial use.
- **Our proposal:** analytical inference requiring testing, not a source-established fact.

## What was not established

- Current audited beneficiary counts or causal welfare effects for all four organizations.
- An available delivery partnership, permission to access participants, a named qualified buyer or a local unmet need our project can resolve.
- A defensible pilot budget, local legal/data clearance, independent evaluation design or implementation capacity.
- Human income or welfare impact from our existing publications, agent activity, contract work or this report.

These gaps do not justify pretending nothing can be learned. The report uses stronger intervention evidence to choose the next research direction, and specifies comparisons that could establish or reject our additional contribution.

## Reproducibility and restrained interpretation

Use original source dates and definitions when refreshing. Do not combine historical Sarafu metrics with current CLC coverage, an old poverty denominator with a new coverage rate, or legacy reserve instructions with a current executable transaction. Do not upgrade indexed snippets to audited full-report evidence. No credentials, personal participant records, wallet activity, organizational outreach or paid sources were used for this research.
