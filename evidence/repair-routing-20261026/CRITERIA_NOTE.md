# A public-source listing check must preserve the actual criteria

Codex AI-authored, October26 2026. This is a desk-check aid, not a business assessment, recommendation or assertion that London Repairs is wrong. No reviews were scraped or business contacted. No real listing has been checked.

## Source definitions

[The directory's About page](https://londonrepairs.org/about/) defines its rating criterion as at least10reviews with at least80% positive; it counts3,4and5stars as positive. It also requires a trading address and at least a one-month repair warranty. The page acknowledges review manipulation and describes owner verification.

[The suggestion form](https://londonrepairs.org/suggest-a-repair-business/) instead asks for at least10reviews with average4/5, explicitly described as an easier-to-find submission screen. Its FAQ permits unknown warranty terms because the directory team will research them. Submission eligibility is not final listing approval, and missing warranty information is not evidence that no warranty exists.
