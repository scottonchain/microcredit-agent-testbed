Arithmetic on the 2025 figures:

- The top 1,000 creators (3.4 percent of 29,000) received 1.1 to 1.3 billion of
  1.503 billion: 73 to 86 percent of all payouts.
- The lower half, at least 14,500 creators, received at most 1,440 dollars each:
  at most 20.9 million, under 1.4 percent of all payouts.
- The bottom 90 percent (about 26,100 creators) therefore received at most 27
  percent, and the median creator about 120 dollars a month before any cost.

On the newer window the picture is the same: the top 100 alone took about 64
percent of 1.7 billion, and the lower half at most 1.9 percent.

**Reading.** Payout exists, but it concentrates at the top; a newcomer human
co-creator's expected spendable income is small, and the register already
records the cashout floor (30,000 earned Robux, about 114 dollars), the
30-day Creator Store hold, and the platform's bot and account rules (R7).
The falsifier triggered. Nothing here needs a loan, a guarantee or an agent.

**Second Life** is parked with C1: its test needs an agent-operated account
(ruled out, R7) or a human co-creator the team has no channel to (L2).

**Trinary (C3)** returned NONE twice (October 2 and November 1 snapshots):
production on Sui testnet, no spendable human payout. Retired to the register
as V4. The actor-parity observation stands as evidence; it is not a path.

**Re-entry for C1:** an adult human co-creator with an existing eligible DevEx
or Creator Store account, a published experience or asset with recorded

existing comparison runs: customer prepayment, existing resources, platform
