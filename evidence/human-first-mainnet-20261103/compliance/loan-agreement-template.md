# Loan agreement (template, plain language, Colorado)

Draft by Claude Code (AI), November 3, 2026, for the D16 pilot under the [legal analysis](../legal-analysis.md). Not legal advice; not in force. Fields in [brackets] are filled per loan by the app from the dossier; nothing here is a solicitation. Door A (business purpose) is the default text; the Door B (consumer purpose) variant is marked.

**Loan agreement**

## 1. Who can borrow

You confirm that you live in Colorado and that you are at least 18 years old. You confirm that you are borrowing for yourself and that no one else will control the money or the account you gave us. One person may have one loan with us at a time.

## 2. What the loan is for

**Door A (business purpose).** You told us the money is for: [purpose from the dossier, in the borrower's words, for example "two sewing machines for my alterations business" or "stock for my market stall"]. You confirm that this loan is for a business, investment or commercial purpose and not primarily for personal, family or household use. We rely on this statement; if it is not true, tell us before you sign.

## 4. How you get the money and how you repay (dollars, never crypto)

**We pay you** in US dollars to [your bank account by ACH, or by instant transfer to the account or handle you gave us: last four digits [xxxx]]. We send it from our own account on [disbursement date]. You never need a crypto wallet, an exchange account or any digital currency to receive or repay this loan.

**You repay us** in US dollars, on or before the due date, by any of: ACH debit from the bank account you authorised in the app; Zelle, Cash App or Venmo to [the lender's receiving handle, shown in the app]; or a card payment link in the app. If you authorise an ACH debit, we will pull $[total repayment] on [due date] unless you have already paid; you can cancel that authorisation at any time by telling us at [contact], and then you pay by one of the other means.

We keep our own records of this loan in a public ledger (a computer program on the Base network) using our own digital dollars. That ledger is ours; it is not an account of yours and you owe nothing to it.

## 5. If you cannot pay on time

Tell us before the due date at [contact]. We will agree a new date in writing for up to [30] more days at the same rate and with no fee. If a loan is 30 days past its due date without an agreed extension, we record it as in default in our ledger; your backer's deposit (section 8) may be used to cover what you owe; and you will not be offered another loan until it is settled. We do not charge late fees, we do not sell debts to collectors, and we do not report to credit bureaus. We may contact you about the overdue amount at the email or phone you gave us, no more than [once a week], and never at your workplace.

## 6. What we promise

We will tell you the cost of this loan in dollars before you accept it (this page), send you the money on the date stated, confirm every payment you make, tell you in writing the main reasons if we ever decline a request from you, and keep what you told us private as described in our privacy notice. We will not charge you anything not listed in section 3.

## 7. Your statements

You confirm that what you told us in the application is true to your knowledge, including the purpose of the loan, where repayment will come from, and the evidence you showed us. You confirm that you are not acting on behalf of anyone else and that no one has paid you or promised you anything to apply.

## 8. Your backer

[Backer's first name and last initial, or "a person who knows you"] has placed a deposit of $[backer amount] with us as a promise that you will repay. You confirm that you know this person and asked them yourself. The backer earns nothing from this loan, not interest and not a fee; if you repay on time their deposit is returned in full; if you default, up to $[amount] of their deposit covers your debt and you then owe that amount to them, not to us. We never ask you to back someone else as a condition of your loan.

[Deposit variant: instead of a backer, you have placed your own deposit of $[amount] with us; it is returned in full with your last payment, or used to cover any default.]

## 9. If the pool pauses or changes

We may stop making new loans at any time. That does not change this loan: your due date, amount and the way you repay stay exactly as written here. If we move our ledger to a different program or address, we will tell you by email and nothing you have to do changes. If the lender of record changes, you will be told in writing who the new lender is and where to pay; your terms do not change.

## 10. Law and disputes

Colorado law governs this agreement. If you have a complaint, write to us first at [contact]; we answer within [10] business days. You may also contact the Colorado Attorney General's Consumer Credit Unit. Nothing in this agreement takes away a right you have under Colorado or federal law.

## 11. Acceptance

By tapping "I accept" in the app on [date and time], you agree to this loan on these terms. The app records your acceptance, the time and a fingerprint (hash) of this exact text, and sends you a copy by email. You may cancel within [1] business day of acceptance by telling us and returning the money if it was already sent, at no cost.

## Notes for the lender of record (not part of the borrower's copy)

- **Door A rests on** C.R.S. 5-1-301(15)(b)(I) (a loan for a business, investment or commercial purpose is not a consumer loan) and 12 CFR 1026.3(a)(1) (Regulation Z exempts business-purpose credit) [primary, legal analysis section 4 and 5]. The purpose statement in section 2 must be the applicant's own words from the dossier, with the evidence tag; do not pre-fill it.
- **Door B rests on** C.R.S. 5-2-201(1) (up to 12 percent a year for a consumer loan that is not a supervised loan), 5-1-301(47) (supervised loan means above 12 percent) and 5-2-301 (licence only for supervised loans) [primary]. The all-in rate including any fee must stay at or below 12 percent; this template charges no fees, so the stated rate is the all-in rate. Count every Door B loan (see [uccc-notification-instructions.md](uccc-notification-instructions.md)): the 26th consumer loan in a calendar year brings Regulation Z's disclosures [primary: 12 CFR 1026.2(a)(17)(v)].
- **Interest deducted at disbursement** is a discount loan; the table shows both the amount sent and the amount repaid in dollars so the cost is visible, which is the disclosure the analysis asks for. For Door B, the stated annual rate is computed on the full principal for the term; a discount changes the effective yield slightly, so keep the stated rate low enough that the effective rate on the amount actually advanced also stays at or below 12 percent (at 9.33 percent nominal on 90 days the effective rate on the advanced amount is about 9.55 percent).
- **Section 4's ledger paragraph** is what makes the compliance design visible to the borrower: the lender's own digital dollars record the loan; the borrower holds none. Do not add any clause that asks the borrower to hold, buy or send USDC.
- **Section 8** implements D7's screen rule (a backer or deposit behind every first loan) and constraint 5 (no yield to backers). The backer signs a separate one-page deposit statement (operations note, November 24) saying they can lose the deposit and earn nothing.
- **Section 5's caps** (30-day extension, no late fee, contact at most weekly, never at work) are the pilot's own policy, written so that the collections practice is stated before the first loan.
- **E-signature.** Acceptance in the app with a hash and a copy by email is the record; keep the hash and the accepted text version in the private records store, not in a public repository.
