## 2. The residual read at source: who may hold the account, and what it binds

The rails table's residual since November 18 was account-type policy and discretion at Kraken and Gemini. Today's reads (plain fetches of the exchanges' own pages, saved and hashed; all `reported`, none `measured`):

**Kraken, "How to get verified for a Business account" (support article 115004862007, last updated August 24, 2026).** Kraken lists the entity types that can open a business account, and "Sole Proprietorships" is on the list beside corporations, LLCs, partnerships and trusts; so the sole-proprietorship branch is admitted on paper by that direct statement. Two document categories are kept distinct on the page: Corporate Formation Documents (certificate of formation, articles of incorporation, certificate of organization, trust deed or equivalent), and the Company Share Registry, whose valid form "may mention a sole member / sole proprietorship and show the name of the owner". Business information (entity name, registration number and date, registered and operating addresses), a proof of operating address dated within three months (no P.O. boxes), and personal identification for every beneficial owner and account accessor are required; a DBA or trade name may be used with the documents that permit it. Two funding restrictions bind stack A directly: bank deposits and withdrawals "can only be transferred to/from a business bank account registered under the same business name", and crypto deposits and withdrawals "must be from a business-owned wallet (no third-party wallets)". Trading fees are the same as for personal accounts. Under "Estimated account creation processing times" the standard is five business days, with the page saying times may increase when further documentation is requested and may be delayed in periods of high demand; an estimate, not a promise.

**Gemini, "Bank Secrecy Act and Anti-money Laundering Compliance Program" (legal page) and "Crypto solutions for businesses" (institutions page).** Before opening an institutional account Gemini collects the institution's legal name, EIN, the full names of all signatories and beneficial owners, the principal place of business, proof of legal existence (state-certified articles of incorporation or certificate of formation, an unexpired business licence, or comparable documents) and government ID for each beneficial owner of ten percent or more and each signatory. The institutions page says a W-9 or W-8, a bank statement and the IDs of all relevant individuals are required for every entity type, with the rest varying by type; it does not name sole proprietorships. The Moonbase entity FAQ returned 403, so which Gemini entity binds a Colorado account remains not established (a search-engine snippet of that FAQ says Gemini Trust keeps New York and Idaho customers and that most other United States customers moved to Gemini Moonbase; a snippet is not a read and is recorded as such).

This is an estimate assembled from two reported durations, not a measured or promised application-to-first-leg time. The personal branch is subject to its own verification and eligibility before the ACH hold; it is not described as carrying only the hold.
